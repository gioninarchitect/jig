# Brand Voice Guidelines — Origin by ILCO Farming

## Brand Personality (5 Traits)

1. **Grounded** — We are farmers first. We speak from the soil, not a boardroom. Real, tangible, honest.
2. **Knowledgeable** — We know cannabis science, SA regulation, and cultivation. We educate without lecturing.
3. **Premium** — Quality over quantity. Every word reflects the care we put into every plant.
4. **Trustworthy** — Transparent about our process, our lab results, our compliance. Nothing to hide.
5. **South African** — Proudly local. We use SA language, reference SA context, serve SA communities.

## Voice Do's and Don'ts

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| "We grow our own cannabis on our farm in Potchefstroom" | "We are a premium cannabis wellness destination" |
| "Lab-tested. Every batch. Here's the certificate." | "Quality you can trust" |
| "Section 21 means your doctor applies to SAHPRA for you" | "Navigate your wellness journey with our Section 21 pathway" |
| "R120/gram for wholesale. Minimum 500g." | "Competitive pricing available on request" |
| "8 collection points across SA" | "Convenient locations nationwide" |
| "Durban Poison — 22% THC, sativa, indoor grown" | "Our finest artisanal craft cannabis selection" |

## Channel Guidelines

**Website**: Lead with facts. Short sentences. Bold key numbers. Every claim backed by evidence.

**Email**: First name personalization. One CTA per email. Subject lines under 45 characters.

**Instagram**: Visual-first. Educational carousels. No product pricing. Farm lifestyle aesthetic.

**LinkedIn**: Industry authority. Compliance updates. B2B case studies. Professional but not stiff.

**WhatsApp**: Quick, helpful, human. Use emojis sparingly. Always include next step.
