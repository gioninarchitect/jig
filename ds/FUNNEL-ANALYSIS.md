# Funnel Analysis — Origin by ILCO Farming

| Stage | Current | Drop-off | Fix |
|-------|---------|----------|-----|
| **Awareness** | Google search, word of mouth | No SEO, no content marketing | Launch blog + Section 21 guide. Run Google Search ads. |
