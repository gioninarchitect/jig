04 — HARVEST
Harvest & Quality Control
Weight recording, quality grading, and lab result tracking.

⚖ Weight Recording

Wet weight, dry weight, trim weight, waste weight — all in grams. Yield percent auto-calculated as (dry/wet) × 100.

🏆 Quality Grading

Three-tier grading: Grade A (premium), Grade B (standard), Grade C (processing grade). Visual badge on each harvest record.

🔬 Lab Testing

Which lab do you use for cannabinoid testing? Do they provide digital results (API/CSV) or paper certificates only? We track: THC%, CBD%, terpene profile, microbiology pass/fail.

📸 Scale Photos

Do you photograph each harvest on the scale? We support photo upload per harvest record (JPEG/PNG/WebP, max 10MB). Required for SAHPRA audit trail?

05 — COMPLIANCE
SAHPRA Compliance & Audit
Built-in compliance scoring engine aligned to Section 22C requirements.

🟢 Compliance Score

5-category scoring: Environmental, Harvest, Batch Tracking, Documentation, Reporting. Traffic-light display (green/amber/red).

📄 Audit Packages

One-click audit package generator. Selectable date range. Includes zone summaries, batch counts, environmental averages, breach history, yield stats.

📅 Document Tracker

Upload and track compliance documents with expiry dates. Auto-alerts when documents approach expiry (30/14/7 days).

📋 Required Documents

Which compliance documents must be tracked? Common: SAHPRA licence, GMP cert, pest management plan, SOPs, insurance, water quality cert, waste disposal agreement.

👥 Compliance Roles

Who is the designated compliance officer? Who can generate audit packages? Current roles: cultivator, farm_manager, compliance_officer. Need additional roles?

07 — REPORTS
Reports & Export
Four standard report types with CSV export. Additional custom reports available on request.

Batch counts by phase, total plants, completions, average cycle time.

🌡 Environmental Report

Average temp/humidity/CO2 per zone, breach counts, compliance percentage.

📊 Yield Report

Yield by strain, average dry weight, yield trends over time, quality grade distribution.

Waste weight totals, waste as % of total harvest, disposal tracking for SAHPRA compliance.
