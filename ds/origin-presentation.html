How It Works

Connected From Day One

Every module talks to the same database. When a sale happens at the POS, stock levels update in real-time for the inventory manager, the owner dashboard, and the customer website.

POS Sale ───> Stock Updated
Stock Updated ───> Owner Dashboard
Stock Updated ───> Website Availability
Low Stock ───> Reorder Alert
Stocktake ───> Variance Report
