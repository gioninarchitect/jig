|---|------|-------|-----------|------------|

## Origin's Unique Position

Origin is the ONLY SA cannabis company that:
1. Grows its own (ILCO Farming)
2. Has track & trace technology (TnT-ZA)
3. Operates 8 collection points
4. Serves B2B wholesale AND B2C retail
5. Has SAHPRA Section 22C cultivation license

No competitor has all 5.

## Opportunities

1. **Content gap**: Most competitors have zero blog content. First-mover advantage in SEO.
2. **B2B**: No dominant wholesale player. Origin can own this space.
3. **Transparency**: None show lab results publicly. Origin can be first.
4. **Technology**: TnT-ZA is a sellable product to other cultivators.
5. **Locations**: 8 points is more than any competitor.

## Threats

1. **Regulatory change**: Cannabis for Private Purposes Act may open market to more players
2. **Price pressure**: Lesotho-grown cannabis is cheaper
3. **Big pharma entry**: Companies like Labat have more capital
4. **Compliance crackdowns**: Any non-compliance could close locations
