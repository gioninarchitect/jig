# Ad Campaigns — Origin by ILCO Farming

## Campaign 1: B2B Wholesale Acquisition

**Objective**: Generate wholesale partner leads
**Platform**: LinkedIn Ads
**Audience**: collection point owners, pharmacy owners, retail entrepreneurs in SA (25-55)
**Budget**: R10,000/month

**Ad Variant A**:
- Headline: "Supply Your collection point with Lab-Tested Cannabis"
- Body: "Origin by ILCO Farming — SA's seed-to-shelf cannabis supplier. COA on every batch. Wholesale pricing from R[X]/gram. 8 collection points."
- CTA: "Request Wholesale Catalog"

**Ad Variant B**:
- Headline: "Wholesale Cannabis — SAHPRA Compliant"
- Body: "Why 50+ SA collection points choose Origin: own cultivation, lab testing, reliable supply, tiered pricing. Apply for a wholesale account."
- CTA: "Apply Now"

**Ad Variant C**:
- Headline: "Your Customers Deserve Tested Cannabis"
- Body: "Every Origin product comes with a Certificate of Analysis. Potency, pesticides, heavy metals — all tested. Wholesale accounts open."
- CTA: "Get Started"

**KPIs**: Cost per lead <R200, 50 leads/month target
**Compliance**: B2B advertising to licensed businesses, not consumers. Compliant.

**Objective**: Build brand recognition
**Platform**: Instagram + Facebook
**Audience**: Cannabis-interested adults 25-45 in Gauteng, Free State, North West
**Budget**: R10,000/month

**Ad Variant A** (Video):
- 15-sec farm tour clip
- Text overlay: "From our farm to your hands"
- CTA: "Follow Us"

**Ad Variant B** (Carousel):
- 4 slides: Farm → Lab → Package → Collection Point
- Caption: "Seed to shelf. Every step tracked."
- CTA: "Learn More"

**Ad Variant C** (Static):
- Photo of cannabis plant with gold ILCO branding
- Text: "Grown in Potchefstroom. Tested in the lab. Available near you."
- CTA: "Find a Location"

**KPIs**: Reach 100,000/month, 5,000 profile visits, 500 follows
