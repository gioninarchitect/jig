01 — Brand Identity

Logo & Brand Mark

The PureGro mark consists of a bold horseshoe arc in bright lime green, a concentric inner arc in dark green, a central water drop, and a gold diamond at the top. Always display on dark backgrounds (#1A1A1A or #0E0E0E). Maintain clear space on all sides. Never stretch, recolour, or place on light backgrounds without approval.
