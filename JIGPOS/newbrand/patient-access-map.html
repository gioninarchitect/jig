Patient-Access / Section 21 — Compliance Foundation Map
GMP Spine

This maps the medical-cannabis patient-access pathway — from consultation to dispensing — to the South African legal instruments that govern it, and to exactly where each requirement is enforced in the Origin software. It is the dispensing-side companion to the GMP governance spine (which covers cultivation → manufacture → release).

⚠ Not legal advice. Foundation skeleton only. Every legal requirement, schedule, validity period, eligibility criterion and fee disclosure below is marked VERIFY and must be confirmed and signed off by a registered South African medicines-law / pharmacy-law specialist before any item is treated as final or shown to real patients as fact. Statute names are cited as the framework, not as confirmed application.
