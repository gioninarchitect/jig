The store, on numbers you can trust

A week of hardening the live Potchefstroom till.
Takings made accurate. VAT corrected to inclusive pricing; pre-launch test transactions removed (the figure dropped from an inflated R6,645 to the true R2,176); card takings now reconcile to the cent against the Capitec terminal.
Day-end & float fixed. Removed the phantom R500 float and the kiosk pop-up bug that stopped Ray closing; shifts now balance and close cleanly.
Stock that behaves. Sales now deduct stock correctly, new products appear on the till instantly, and the brand-first Wellness layout with product images is live for staff.
Reporting. A new Takings Report — any date range, cash/card split, daily breakdown, CSV/PDF — with email to stakeholders.

The medical & compliance layer

Building the regulated foundation under the retail.
Patient portal & Section 21 gate. Online medical orders are now gated behind Section 21 status and prescription upload, with a pharmacist review queue — grounded to a legal-foundation map.
Compliance tooling continues to mature — POPIA data-consent layer, SOP library and GMP inspection-readiness, keeping us audit-ready for SAHPRA.

Farm ↔ retail connected

One view from cultivation to till.
Owner Dashboard (TnT-ZA). A combined farm + retail view, with the Potchefstroom store's live takings pulled into the farm platform via a secure bridge.

The national network engine

The week's headline — how Origin scales past one store.
Outlet onboarding system. A document-gated wizard for pharmacies to join Origin — verifies SAPC premises & Responsible Pharmacist, company & banking. The medical-cannabis authority (SAHPRA 22A / Section 21) stays with ILCO Farming; a joining pharmacy only proves it's a licensed pharmacy.
One-click activation. An admin review screen checks the documents, switches the outlet's own branch-scoped POS live and seeds the product catalogue — ready to trade.
Branch #2 queued: Röscher Pharmacy, Kroonstad — loaded and ready to onboard as the first network pharmacy dispensing ILCO medical cannabis.

Origin Botanica — a farm-to-skin cosmetic range proposal, turning post-trim farm material into a high-margin house range the whole network can carry.
