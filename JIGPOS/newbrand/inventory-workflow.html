Stock Take to POS Flow

1

Create Stock Take Session

Manager creates a new session for the branch. Products are loaded with current expected quantities.

2

Staff Counts Stock

Staff physically counts each product and enters actual quantities. Photos are required for verification.

3

Submit for Review

Once all items are counted, the session is submitted. System calculates variances automatically.

4

Manager Approval

Manager reviews variances and approves the stock take. Large variances may require investigation.

5

Inventory Updates

Upon approval, branch inventory is automatically updated to match actual counted quantities.

6

POS Reflects Changes

POS immediately shows updated stock levels. Out-of-stock items are automatically hidden.

Instant Synchronization

When a stock take is approved, inventory updates happen immediately. The POS system reads from the same database, so staff will see accurate stock levels within seconds of approval.

Key Benefits

Accuracy

Photo verification and manager approval ensure stock counts are accurate before updating inventory.

Real-Time

Changes sync instantly across all systems. No delays between stock take approval and POS updates.

Visibility

Owners can view stock levels, variances, and performance across all branches from anywhere.

No Overselling

Out-of-stock items are automatically hidden from POS, preventing sales of unavailable products.
