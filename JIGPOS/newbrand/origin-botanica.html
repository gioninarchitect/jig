What we can make from trim

Grounded in standard post-harvest valorisation — trim & leaf decarbed and infused into a carrier, then blended with African botanical bases.
  • Infused oils & butters — sugar/fan leaf decarbed into a carrier oil base for balms, butters and salves.
  • Botanical & aroma extracts — terpene-rich leaf for scent and a botanical "actives" story (Tier 1, no cannabinoid dosing).
  • CBD-rich extract — fan leaf runs higher in CBD; the route to the Tier 2 "active" line (regulatory gating below).
  • Bases that pair well — hemp-seed oil, shea, cocoa & marula butter, coconut oil, beeswax, vitamin E.

Sources: GAIACA, Triminator, Veriheal & Dutch Passion on trim/leaf valorisation; Humblebee & Me / Bottega on hemp-seed-oil formulations.

Regulatory posture ⚠ VERIFY

Grounded in SAHPRA's published scheduling — but treat every cannabinoid claim as placeholder until a medicines-law specialist signs off.
Tier 1 (Origin Botanica) — clear to sell. No cannabinoid actives, hemp-seed oil & botanicals only, cosmetic claims → an ordinary cosmetic, sellable nationwide.
Tier 2/3 (CBD & medical) — VERIFY before any sale. SAHPRA lists CBD as Schedule 4, except: low-dose complementary medicines (≤600 mg CBD per pack, ≤20 mg/day) and processed plant material with ≤0.001% THC / ≤0.0075% CBD. A CBD cosmetic falls in a grey zone — it defaults to Schedule 4 unless it sits inside those envelopes. No CBD/medical SKU goes live without written specialist sign-off.

Source: SAHPRA — THC & CBD scheduling (Govt Notice 586, May 2020) and SAHPRA industry communication.
