Your Patient Journey

Step 1: Request Consultation

Complete our simple intake form with your medical history and condition details. Our team reviews your information to match you with the right healthcare provider.

5 minutes

Step 2: Doctor Consultation

Meet with a licensed doctor via secure video call. They'll review your medical history, discuss your symptoms, and determine if medical cannabis is appropriate for your treatment.

30-45 minutes

Step 3: Section 21 Authorization

If approved, your doctor issues a Section 21 Authorization Letter - the legal document that allows you to access medical cannabis in South Africa. Valid for 180 days.

Same day

Same day

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a consultation cost?

The consultation fee is R350, which includes your eligibility assessment, doctor consultation, and if approved, your Section 21 Authorization Letter.

Do I need a referral from my GP?

No referral is required. You can book directly with our medical team. However, bringing any relevant medical records or test results can help your consultation.

How long is the authorization valid?

Section 21 Authorization Letters are valid for 180 days (6 months). We'll remind you when it's time to renew and help schedule your follow-up consultation.

Is the consultation confidential?

Absolutely. All consultations are strictly confidential and protected by doctor-patient privilege. Your medical information is encrypted and never shared without your consent.

What if I'm not approved?

Not everyone qualifies for medical cannabis. If the doctor determines it's not suitable for your condition, they'll discuss alternative treatment options. The consultation fee is non-refundable as it covers the doctor's time and assessment.
