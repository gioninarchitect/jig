The 8 Principles

Every GMP Principle — Mapped to Origin

The same framework your infographic covers, enforced line-by-line in the platform.

🧼
Principle 01

Hygiene & Cleanliness

Origin Platform Does This

Pre-departure vehicle checklist (GPP 2.7.5) is mandatory before every dispatch. Storage temperature monitoring flags any breach before product leaves the facility. No checklist = no dispatch authorised.

📋
Principle 02

Documentation

Origin Platform Does This

Every dispensing action is logged with timestamp, staff ID, batch number and COA reference. Auto-generated BMR equivalent. No action is unrecorded — the system writes the trail for you.

✅
Principle 03

Validation

Origin Platform Does This

Batch COA must be uploaded and QA-approved before a product enters inventory. System blocks unapproved batches at the gate — no manual override without RP sign-off.

🎓
Principle 04

Qualification

Origin Platform Does This

Staff roles are gated by SAPC registration status. Interns operate within a supervised scope only. Schedule 6 dispensing requires RP countersignature — the system will not allow it otherwise.

🌡️
Principle 05

Calibration

Origin Platform Does This

Temperature and humidity monitoring triggers GWDP spec flags at 22 °C max and 50–55 % RH. Out-of-range readings generate automatic alerts and lock affected stock for QA review.

📚
Principle 06

Training

Origin Platform Does This

Role-based training modules tied to SAPC competency log. When a deviation is detected the platform automatically assigns the relevant training ticket to the staff member before they can resume that task.

🔒
Principle 07

Change Control

Origin Platform Does This

All product and procedure changes require QA approval before taking effect. Admin-controlled change queue. Nothing is silent — every change carries an author, timestamp and reason.

🔍
Principle 08

Audit

Origin Platform Does This

ComplianceLog captures every system action with PASS / WARN / BLOCK status. Exportable audit package — SAHPRA-ready format. One click: complete site audit history.

Legal Foundation

Built on South African Medicines Law

Origin's GMP framework is grounded in the Acts that govern every South African pharmacy.

⚖️
Pharmacy Act 53 of 1974

Good Pharmacy Practice (GPP) Rule 2.7.5 imposes specific transport and handling obligations on every dispensary. Origin's pre-departure checklist, chain-of-custody log and cold-chain monitoring implement these requirements at the system level, removing reliance on manual compliance.

GPP Rule 2.7.5 · Transport Obligations
📜
Medicines Act 101 of 1965

Section 22A governs Schedule 6 (and other scheduled) substances, requiring full chain of custody. Sections 38 and 39 set handling and storage standards. Origin enforces RP countersignature at every Schedule 6 dispensing event and logs each step immutably.

S22A · Schedule 6 Chain of Custody · S38 / S39
🏛️
SAHPRA GMP Guidelines

SAHPRA's GMP guidelines require documented cultivation batch records, verified COAs and traceable dispensing histories. Origin's CultivationBatch model, mandatory COA gate and ComplianceLog export are specifically designed to satisfy a SAHPRA inspection with minimal preparation time.
