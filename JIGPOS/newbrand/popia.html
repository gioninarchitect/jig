Data Subject Access Request (DSAR)

POPIA §23 — a person may ask what personal information we hold about them. Enter their email to retrieve their profile and transaction history.

Right to Erasure / Deletion

POPIA §24 — a data subject may request deletion of their personal information. We anonymise the person and their records (transactions are kept but de-identified for tax/accounting). Staff accounts are protected and cannot be erased here.

The 8 POPIA Conditions for Lawful Processing

Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 — the conditions Origin processes personal information under.
Information Officer: the responsible person registered with the Information Regulator. Requests above are logged. Retention: customer/transaction records are kept for the period required by tax & pharmacy law, then de-identified.
⚠ Exact retention periods and the registered Information Officer must be confirmed with your POPIA/legal adviser before this is treated as final policy.
