Staff Training Reference Guide

POS Sale Flow

Cash Payment
1
Add Items to Cart
Scan or select products from the product grid. Quantities can be adjusted using +/- buttons.
2
Review Cart Total
Verify all items and amounts are correct before proceeding.
3
Select "Cash" Payment
Click the CASH button in the payment methods section.
4
Enter Amount Tendered
Enter the cash amount received from customer. System calculates change automatically.
Amount Tendered: R 500.00 | Change Due: R 127.50
5
Complete Sale
Click "COMPLETE CASH SALE" to finalize. Receipt prints automatically.
Cash drawer opens automatically on completion. Count change back to customer.
Card Payment (Speedpoint)
1
Add Items to Cart
Scan or select products. Review cart total.
2
Select "Card" Payment
Click the CARD button in payment methods.
3
Process on Speedpoint Terminal
Enter the amount on the Speedpoint device. Customer taps/inserts card and enters PIN.
4
Wait for Approval
Speedpoint will print a receipt with reference number when approved.
5
Enter Reference Number
Enter the Speedpoint reference number from the receipt into the POS.
Reference: SP-2026020412345 | Notes: Visa Credit
6
Complete Card Sale
Click "COMPLETE CARD SALE" to finalize transaction.
Important: Always enter the Speedpoint reference for reconciliation. Never complete without it.
EFT / Bank Transfer
Important: EFT payments require Admin/Manager approval before goods are released.

Staff at POS

1
Add Items to Cart
Build the order as normal.
2
Select "Manual EFT" Payment
Click the EFT button in payment methods.
3
Show Banking Details to Customer
POS displays bank account details for customer to make transfer.
Bank: Standard Bank | Account: Origin by ILCO Farming | Acc #: 123456789 | Branch: 051001
4
Customer Makes Transfer
Customer uses their banking app to make the EFT payment using the reference provided.
5
Upload Proof of Payment
Customer shows POP on their phone. Staff takes screenshot/photo and uploads via the POS file upload.
6
Submit for Approval
Click "UPLOAD & SUBMIT". Order goes to pending approval status.

Admin/Manager Approval

7
View Pending Orders
Admin/Manager goes to Orders tab in Admin Panel. Pending EFT orders show "View POP" link.
8
Review Proof of Payment
Click "View POP" to see the uploaded proof. Verify amount, date, and reference match.
9
Approve or Reject
Click "Approve" to confirm payment and release order. Click "Reject" if POP is invalid.
Do NOT release goods until approval is confirmed. EFT payments are async - wait for manager approval before customer leaves with products.
Split Payment
1
Add Items to Cart
Build complete order with all items.
2
Select "Split" Payment
Click SPLIT button to enter split payment mode.
3
Enter First Payment Amount
Enter the amount for the first payment method (e.g., R200 cash).
4
Process First Payment
Complete the first portion (cash or card as per method selected).
5
Process Remaining Balance
System shows remaining amount. Select method and complete second payment.
Total: R 450.00 | Paid: R 200.00 (Cash) | Remaining: R 250.00
6
Finalize Sale
Once balance is R0.00, sale completes automatically.
Split payments can combine any methods: Cash + Card, Card + EFT, etc.

Till Session Flow

Opening a Till
1
Log In to POS
Use your OTP login credentials to access the system.
2
Select Till Terminal
Choose the till you're operating (Till 1, Till 2, etc.).
3
Count Float
Count the cash float in the drawer. Standard float is R500.
4
Enter Opening Balance
Enter the counted float amount into the system.
Opening Float: R 500.00
5
Confirm Opening
Click "OPEN TILL" to start your session. System records timestamp.
Always verify float! Report discrepancies to manager before opening.
Closing a Till
1
Complete All Pending Sales
Ensure no sales are in progress. Process or void any pending transactions.
2
Initiate Till Close
Click "CLOSE TILL" button in the POS menu.
3
Count Cash in Drawer
Count all cash by denomination. Be thorough and accurate.

Coins

Count R5, R2, R1, 50c, 20c, 10c separately

Notes

Count R200, R100, R50, R20, R10 separately

4
Enter Counted Amount
Enter your total counted cash. System compares to expected.
Expected: R 3,245.50 | Counted: R 3,245.50 | Variance: R 0.00
5
Review Session Summary
System shows total sales, payment breakdown, and any variances.
6
Submit Till Close
Click "SUBMIT" to close the till. Print Z-report if required.
Variances over R50 require manager sign-off and explanation note.

Daily Cashup Flow

Manager Cashup Process
1
Ensure All Tills Closed
All operator tills must be closed before daily cashup can begin.
2
Access Cashup Module
Go to Admin > Cashup or Manager Dashboard > End of Day.
3
Review Till Summaries
System shows aggregated data from all closed tills.

Cash Sales

Total cash across all tills

Card Sales

Total Speedpoint transactions

EFT Sales

Total bank transfers received

4
Physical Cash Count
Count all cash from tills combined. Separate float for next day.
Expected Total: R 8,450.00 | Float Reserved: R 500.00 | Banking: R 7,950.00
5
Enter Counted Amounts
Enter actual counted amounts for each payment method.
6
Reconcile Card Transactions
Match Speedpoint batch totals with POS card sales.
7
Add Notes for Variances
Document any discrepancies with explanations.
8
Submit for Approval
Click "SUBMIT CASHUP" to send to owner/HQ for approval.
Owner/HQ Approval
1
Review Submitted Cashup
Access pending cashups from Admin Dashboard > Approvals.
2
Verify Totals
Compare system sales vs counted amounts. Review any variances.
3
Check Supporting Documents
Review attached notes, photos, or Speedpoint batch slips if uploaded.
4
Approve or Reject
Approve to finalize day, or reject with notes for manager to correct.

Approve

Day is closed. Banking amount confirmed.

Reject

Sent back to manager with notes. Must recount and resubmit.

Stock Take Flow

Creating a Stock Take Session
1
Log In via OTP
Access the Stock Take app using your branch email and OTP code.
2
Select Stock Take Type
Choose the type of count to perform.

Full Count

All products in inventory

Cycle Count

Selected categories only

Spot Check

Random selection of products

3
Create Session
Click "CREATE" to generate stock take session. System assigns a session number.
Session: ST-20260204-001 | Type: Full Count | Items: 151
Counting Products
1
Select Product
Choose a product from the list or scan barcode.
2
Physical Count
Count or weigh the product. For weighables, use calibrated scale.
3
Take Photo (Required)
Photograph the scale reading or counted items. This is mandatory for compliance.
SA GMP Requirement: Photo verification is mandatory for all counts.
4
Enter Actual Quantity
Input the counted/weighed amount. System calculates variance automatically.
Expected: 250g | Actual: 245g | Variance: -5g (-2%)
5
Add Notes (if variance)
For variances >5%, add explanation notes.
6
Save Item
Click "SAVE" to record count. Move to next item.
Submitting for Review
1
Complete All Items
Ensure all products have been counted with photos uploaded.
2
Review Summary
Check the stock take summary for completeness.

Items Counted

151/151 complete

Variances

3 items with variance >5%

Photos

151/151 uploaded

3
Submit for Manager Review
Click "SUBMIT FOR REVIEW" to send to manager.
4
Manager Review
Manager reviews variances and approves or requests recount.
5
Final Approval
Once approved, inventory is updated to match counted quantities.
Compliance: Approved stock takes generate SAHPRA-compliant reports automatically.
