01

Executive Summary

We propose a complete, turnkey cannabis dispensary management platform designed specifically for South African compliance requirements. This solution includes Point of Sale, Inventory Management, Stock Take with photo verification, and multi-location support.

The platform is currently live and operational, demonstrating proven reliability and real-world functionality across multiple dispensary locations.

Key Differentiators: Section 21 compliance built-in, OCR-powered stock take with scale photo verification, real-time inventory sync, mobile-first design, and complete white-label customization with your branding.

02

Platform Features

Point of Sale

Fast, intuitive sales interface with category filtering, quick-add buttons, and multiple payment methods including cash, card, and split payments.

Stock Take App

Mobile stock counting with photo verification, OCR scale reading, variance tracking, and manager approval workflow.

Inventory Management

Real-time stock levels, low stock alerts, multi-location support, and automatic POS sync when stock is updated.

Owner Dashboard

Sales analytics, staff performance, inventory overview, and variance reports across all collection points in one view.

Admin Panel

Product management, user roles, branch configuration, and system settings all in one centralized location.

Section 21 Compliance

Medical cannabis tracking, prescription verification, daily limits, and compliance reporting built-in.
