GMP Principle Map

How Origin Enforces Each GMP Requirement

Each principle from your GMP framework maps directly to a platform feature. This is not interpretation — it is engineering.

01
GMP § SOPs

Standard Operating Procedures

Every activity must be governed by a documented, approved SOP. Staff must have access and prove understanding.

Origin Implementation
Role-Based SOP Library
Each role (RP, Intern, Driver, Floor Staff, QA) has a curated SOP library. Staff must digitally acknowledge each SOP before their workflow unlocks. Acknowledgement is timestamped and co-signed by manager. No acknowledgement = no access.
digital-sign-off role-gated version-controlled
02
GMP § Deviations

Deviation Detection & Reporting

Any departure from an approved procedure must be identified, recorded, and investigated before the process continues.

Origin Implementation
Auto-CAPA Trigger Engine
When staff deviate from SOP — stock take without photo, delivery without temperature log, dispense without S21 verification — the system automatically raises a CAPA ticket, logs the deviation with evidence, and notifies the QA Manager and RP in real time.
auto-detect real-time-alert evidence-capture
03
GMP § CAPA

Corrective & Preventive Action

Root cause must be addressed. Preventive action documented. Effectiveness verified before closure.

Origin Implementation
Gated CAPA Ticket System
A CAPA ticket auto-assigns a specific training module to the responsible staff member. The ticket is locked until: (1) staff completes the module, (2) QA manager counter-signs, (3) a 14-day monitoring period passes without recurrence. Only then does the ticket close.
dual-countersign training-linked monitoring-window
04
GMP § Change Control

Change Control & Approval Gate

All changes to product, process, or procedure must be formally approved and trailed before implementation.

Origin Implementation
Admin-Gated Change Workflow
No product description, pricing, protocol, or SOP can be changed unilaterally. All changes require RP + Admin dual approval, are staged in a pending state with a diff view, and only go live after approval. Every change is permanently recorded in the audit trail with approver, timestamp, and justification.
dual-approval diff-trail staged-deploy
05
GMP § Documentation

Complete Documentation System

If it isn't documented, it didn't happen. Every step, batch, dispense, and decision must be recorded and retrievable.

Origin Implementation
Full Document Chain
COA per batch (Certificate of Analysis), PIL per dispensing order (Patient Information Leaflet), BMR equivalent (batch dispensing record), S21 approval records — all generated, linked, stored, and exportable as PDF with one click. Exportable to SAHPRA, SAPC, or internal QA formats.
COA-chain PIL-linked BMR-equivalent PDF-export
06
GMP § Training

Role-Based Training & Competency

All personnel must be trained on their specific roles and responsibilities. Competency must be evidenced and refreshed.

Origin Implementation
Training Hub with Logbook
Every role has a curated training module sequence. Intern pharmacists have a SAPC competency logbook built into the platform, tracking CPD hours, supervisor sign-offs, and required rotations. All completions logged with timestamp, score, and trainer name. Retraining triggers automatically after CAPA.
SAPC-logbook CPD-hours trainer-signed
07
GMP § Audit Trail

Immutable Audit Trail

A complete, tamper-evident record of all GMP-significant events, with who, what, when, and outcome.

Origin Implementation
ComplianceLog — Every Action Traced
Every system action writes to ComplianceLog: staff ID, action type, timestamp, result (PASS / WARNING / BLOCKED), linked SOP, linked law. The log is append-only, exportable, and filterable by role, date range, or SOP reference. Nothing can be deleted. Everything can be exported for SAPC inspection.
append-only PASS/WARN/BLOCK SOP-linked inspection-ready
08
GMP § Internal Audit

QA Dashboard & Internal Audit Tools

Regular internal audits verify that GMP is being followed. QA has independent oversight of all operations.

Origin Implementation
QA Manager Dashboard
Keke's dashboard shows: compliance rate per role (today / 7-day / 30-day), all open CAPAs with age and assignee, overdue trainings with escalation status, SOP acknowledgment rates per staff member, and a one-click audit export for any inspection period. The QA role cannot be overridden by operations management.

CAPA Workflow

SOP Violation → CAPA → Close-Out

From detection to documented resolution — no step can be skipped, no ticket closed without evidence.

VIOLATION
STEP 01
SOP Deviation Occurs
Staff performs action that departs from approved SOP — system detects missing required field, photo, or verification.
AUTO
STEP 02
System Auto-Detects
Action blocked or flagged. Evidence captured automatically. QA Manager and RP notified in real time via platform alert.
OPEN
STEP 03
CAPA Ticket Raised
CAPA ticket auto-generated with deviation type, staff member, SOP reference, and root cause classification. Training module assigned.
IN PROGRESS
STEP 04
Training Completed
Staff completes the assigned SOP module and passes the assessment. Score recorded. Manager receives notification to countersign.
CLOSED
STEP 05
QA Counter-Signs & Closes
QA Manager countersigns. 14-day monitoring window starts. No recurrence = ticket closed. All steps permanently recorded in ComplianceLog.

Legal Grounding

Statutory Compliance Anchor Matrix

Origin Platform GMP enforcement is anchored to South African pharmaceutical law. Every GMP feature maps to a specific statutory provision.
Note: Specific regulatory interpretations are subject to confirmation by a qualified medicines law specialist.

Act 53 of 1974
Pharmacy Act
Republic of South Africa · as amended
  • Section 22C — Responsible Pharmacist obligations enforced via RP-gated workflow controls and all dispensing requiring RP acknowledgement or supervision flag.
  • Section 35(1) — Compulsory GMP standards mapped to platform CAPA engine and SOP acknowledgement system.
  • SAPC GPP Rule 2.7.5 — Good Pharmacy Practice documentation requirements met by full COA/PIL/BMR document chain.
Act 101 of 1965
Medicines & Related Substances Act
Republic of South Africa · as amended
  • Section 21 — Authorised use of unregistered medicines. Platform enforces S21 verification before any Sch 5/6 dispense; hard-block if authorisation not on file.
  • Scheduling Regulations — Scheduling status enforced at product level; floor staff cannot access Sch 4+ without RP counter-sign in the transaction record.
  • Record-Keeping (Reg. 27) — All dispensing records stored with batch, patient, authorising practitioner, and timestamp. Minimum 5-year retention enforced.
SAHPRA
SA Health Products Regulatory Authority
Inspection & Enforcement Framework
  • GMP Inspection Readiness — Audit trail export generates SAHPRA-formatted inspection pack: SOPs, training records, CAPA history, batch records, temperature logs.
  • Licence Condition Compliance — Each branch's 22C licence conditions are loaded as platform constraints; deviations auto-raise critical CAPA tickets.
  • Defect Reporting — Product defect/recall triggers a platform-wide communication to all dispensing staff and auto-quarantines affected batch stock.
SAPC Y-Number
South African Pharmacy Council
CPD Requirement & Intern Training Standards
  • Intern Training Logbook — SAPC-aligned logbook built into platform. Supervisor signs off competency blocks digitally. Full audit trail exportable for intern registration.
  • CPD Hour Tracking — All training module completions logged as CPD-eligible hours with module category, facilitator, and outcome score.
  • Professional Conduct — SAPC Code of Conduct obligations built into onboarding declaration; staff acknowledge Code alongside role SOPs at activation.
