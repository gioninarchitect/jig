7.1
Intern Placement Programme

Origin applies for SAPC accreditation as a community pharmacy internship site, enabling it to host final-year and post-graduate intern pharmacists on their mandatory community pharmacy rotation (6-month blocks).

  • SAPC Accreditation: Origin registers each node as an approved community pharmacy internship site under SAPC Board Notice requirements.
  • University Partners: University of Pretoria, University of the Witwatersrand, Sefako Makgatho University (SMU), University of the Western Cape (UWC).
  • Node Capacity: Each Origin node hosts 1–2 interns per rotation under direct RP supervision at all times.
  • Stipend: Approximately R8,000–R12,000/month per intern vs. R25,000/month for a full RP salary — a meaningful operational saving at scale without compromising supervised-practice standards.
  • Financial Impact: At 10 nodes hosting 1 intern each at mid-range R10,000: potential R130,000–R170,000/month reduction in salary overhead (partial RP hours offset, not full substitution).
7.2
Intern Scope of Practice at Origin Nodes
All intern activities at Origin nodes are performed under the direct oversight and countersignature authority of the site's Responsible Pharmacist (RP). No intern may act independently on any Schedule 6 substance.
Permitted Under RP Supervision
Patient counseling on OTC and supplement products
Preparation of patient-ready packs (sealed, labeled) under RP oversight
Section 21 prescription processing — initiation only; RP countersigns
PIL (Patient Information Leaflet) preparation and assembly
Stock take and inventory management activities
Patient onboarding and medical history capture in the digital platform
Not Permitted Independently
Independent dispensing of any Schedule 6 substance (cannabis products)
Signing off on any dispensing record without RP countersignature
Authorizing Section 21 prescriptions
Releasing GMP-certified batch inventory from vault
7.3
Digital Competency Logbook

SAPC requires intern pharmacists to maintain a detailed competency logbook throughout their rotation. Origin integrates this requirement directly into the platform's staff module.

  • Logbook Tracking: SAPC-mandated intern logbook requirements are tracked within the Origin platform's staff management module, eliminating paper-based logbooks.
  • Competency Alignment: Modules aligned with SAPC's Community Pharmacy rotation requirements — dispensing, patient counselling, stock management, clinical documentation.
  • Sign-Off Workflow: Each competency entry records supervisor sign-off (RP digital signature), date, and observation notes — creating an auditable trail.
  • Export: Auto-generated logbook PDF export at end of rotation, formatted to SAPC submission standards.

Logistics & Cold-Chain Compliance
Driver GPP Compliance Protocol
Operational framework governing the transport, delivery, and chain-of-custody handling of pharmaceutical products by Origin delivery drivers as unregistered persons under the Medicines Act.
Statutory Authority — Multiple Instruments
GPP Rule 2.7.5 · Medicines Act S38 & S39 · GNR.1158 Reg 21 · Section 22A
SAPC GPP Rule 2.7.5 — Rules Relating to Good Pharmacy Practice (Board Notice 49 of 2015, Government Gazette No. 38511) governs pharmaceutical delivery operations.

Sections 38 & 39 of Medicines Act 101 of 1965 — define "handling by unregistered persons" and the permitted scope thereof.

Regulation 21 of GNR. 1158 — Practice of Pharmacy regulations applicable to every person in the delivery chain.

Section 22A of Act 101 — Schedule 6 chain-of-custody and tracking requirements for cannabis products at all points of the supply chain.
8.1
Driver Legal Status
Origin delivery drivers are UNREGISTERED PERSONS under the Medicines Act. This status is fixed — no internal designation or employment contract can alter their statutory classification.
  • May transport pre-packed, sealed, labeled medicine parcels prepared and authorized by the RP.
  • May NOT handle, open, repackage, or store bulk or unsealed medicines under any circumstances.
  • Must carry and deliver the Patient Information Leaflet (PIL) with every pharmaceutical parcel.
  • Must carry written pharmacist instructions specific to each parcel at the time of delivery.

8.4
Delivery Confirmation Protocol Rule 2.7.5.2
  • Adult Recipient Verification: Driver must confirm an adult recipient is present and that their ID matches the patient record in the driver app before handover.
  • Digital Signature: Recipient signs digitally in the driver app (or physically on a manifest copy) at point of delivery — logged with timestamp and GPS coordinate.
  • Failed Delivery Trigger: If no adult is present, the Failed Delivery Protocol (§8.5) activates immediately — medicine cannot be left unattended.

A driver who leaves medicine unattended at a premises with no adult recipient is in direct violation of GPP Rule 2.7.5.2(d) and exposes Origin's SAPC licence to sanction. The driver app technically prevents this by locking "Delivered" status unless recipient ID verification is completed.
8.6
Schedule 6 Chain of Custody Section 22A
  • Batch-Level Tracking: Every Schedule 6 unit (cannabis flower, pre-rolls, extracts) is tracked by batch number from pharmacy vault dispatch to confirmed patient receipt — no break in chain.
  • Custody Record: Driver app generates a timestamped, GPS-tagged chain of custody record at the moment of delivery confirmation — transmitted to the Origin compliance ledger.
  • Loss / Theft Reporting: Any loss, damage, or theft must be reported to the branch RP immediately and logged in the driver app with a mandatory incident description. RP is responsible for onward SAPC/SAHPRA reporting obligations.

Platform Intelligence
AI-Assisted Role Compliance Engine
Every staff member has an AI assistant that knows their role, KPIs, SOPs, and the relevant SA law. When an action deviates from protocol, the system auto-creates a training ticket — closing the loop between operational deviation and corrective learning.
Regulatory Basis
SAPC GPP Rules · SAHPRA GMP Guidelines · GNR.1158 · Consumer Protection Act
The Compliance Engine operationalises documentary requirements from SAPC GPP Board Notice 49/2015, SAHPRA GMP Guidelines, GNR.1158 Practice of Pharmacy Regulations, and the Consumer Protection Act into a real-time role-level audit mechanism. Each role's context is bounded by its statutory authority — the AI assistant enforces those bounds at the point of action.
9.1
Engine Architecture

The compliance engine operates as an event-driven layer on top of every platform action. Each action is evaluated against the role's SOP ruleset in real-time. Passing actions are logged to the compliance ledger. Deviating actions trigger a CAPA ticket assigned to the relevant SOP module.
