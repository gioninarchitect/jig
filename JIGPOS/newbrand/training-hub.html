Point of Sale (POS)
Processing walk-in customer transactions
Not Started
1

Starting a Transaction

To process a walk-in sale:

  • Navigate to POS section
  • Browse or search for products
  • Click products to add them to the cart
  • Adjust quantities as needed
2

Customer Information

Capture customer details for the receipt:

  • Name (optional but recommended)
  • Email (for digital receipt)
  • Phone number
3

Payment Methods

Accept multiple payment types:

  • Cash - Enter amount received, system calculates change
  • Card - Process via card terminal
  • EFT - Bank transfer reference required
  • InstaPay - QR code instant payment
4

Completing the Sale

After payment confirmation, a Sale Complete Modal appears with options:

  • Email Receipt - Send SA-standard tax invoice to customer's email
  • Print Receipt - Print receipt for the customer
  • Download PDF - Save invoice/receipt as PDF
  • New Sale - Clear cart and start fresh

Stock is automatically deducted and the transaction appears in sales history.

5

Email Receipts

Email receipts are SA tax invoice compliant and include:

  • VAT registration number and business details
  • Line items with Description, Qty, Unit Price, Amount
  • Subtotal (excluding VAT)
  • VAT (15%)
  • Total (including VAT)
Best Practice

Always offer to email the receipt - it provides proof of purchase and reduces paper waste. Toast notifications confirm email delivery.

Section 21 Medical Cannabis
Third-party prescription verification integration
Not Started
1

About Section 21

Section 21 is South Africa's legal framework for medical cannabis access:

  • Patients require authorisation from a licensed healthcare provider
  • Prescriptions are valid for specific products and quantities
  • Verification is handled by a third-party integration
  • Origin by ILCO Farming does not manage the verification process
2

Admin Review Process

When patients upload documents:

  • Documents appear in the Section 21 pending queue
  • Third-party service verifies authenticity
  • Approved patients gain access to medical products
  • Rejected applications receive notification with reason
3

Expiry Monitoring

The system tracks authorisation validity:

  • Authorisation letters valid for 180 days
  • Expiring documents flagged 30 days before expiry
  • Patients notified to renew documentation
  • Access revoked automatically upon expiry
Integration Note

Section 21 verification is handled externally. Contact your system administrator for integration configuration or issues.

Understanding Section 21
Legal framework for medical cannabis
Not Started
1

What is Section 21?

Section 21 of the Medicines Act allows for the use of unregistered medicines (including cannabis) for individual patients when prescribed by a healthcare provider.

2

Requirements

  • Consultation with a registered healthcare provider
  • Medical indication for cannabis treatment
  • Section 21 authorisation letter from your doctor
  • Valid prescription for specific products
Important

Section 21 verification is handled by a third-party medical verification service integrated into our platform.

Uploading Documents
Two-step verification process
Not Started
1

Step 1: Authorisation Letter

  • Log into your dashboard
  • Navigate to Section 21 card
  • Upload your authorisation letter (PDF or image)
  • Enter your doctor's details
  • Wait for approval (24-48 hours)
2

Step 2: Prescription

  • After authorisation approval, upload prescription
  • Prescription must list specific products
  • Wait for prescription approval
  • Gain access to medical products
Validity

Authorisation letters are valid for 180 days. You will receive renewal reminders before expiry.
