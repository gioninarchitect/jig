Auditable Pharmacy Pickup

Origin Retail keeps the Section 21 workflow in place and adds partner pharmacies as controlled pickup and dispensing points. The backend now links online payment, pharmacy handling fees, package custody, central batch inventory, returns, refunds, settlements, and audit evidence.

Value Message

Origin Retail gives stakeholders one controlled place to manage Section 21 pharmacy pickup, dispensing partner accountability, stock movement, payment evidence and audit readiness without relying on email chains or manual trackers.

What Is Now In Scope

Partner pharmacy onboarding with required document approval before activation.
Section 21 order controls including prescription and prescriber references.
Batch inventory movement for pack and return-to-hub actions.
OTP collection at the pharmacy counter with named actor and custody event.
Financial ledger for collection fees, settlements and refund tracking.

What UAT Must Prove

Every CTA writes to the database.
No stock adjustment happens without an inventory movement row.
No pharmacy fee is earned without a ledger row.
No return is accepted without waybill, refund and custody evidence.
Auditor review can reconstruct the full order lifecycle.

Workflow Accordions

Production value: prevents unapproved pickup points from receiving Section 21 packages.
Production value: keeps the medical authorization workflow as the gate before pharmacy fulfillment.
Production value: financial and physical stock records stay aligned.
Production value: chain of custody is visible for operations, pharmacy partners and auditors.
Production value: uncollected orders stop sitting in limbo and reverse logistics becomes auditable.
Production value: partner fees and refunds can be reconciled from immutable source records.
