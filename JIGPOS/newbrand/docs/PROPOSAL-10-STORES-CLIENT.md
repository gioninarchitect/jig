## What You Get

### Point of Sale (POS)
Fast, intuitive, works offline when internet drops. Your staff can process sales in seconds.

- Cash, Card (Speedpoint), EFT payments
- Split payments
- Automatic stock deduction
- Receipt printing & email

### Stock Management
Know exactly what you have, where it is, and when it's running low.

- Real-time inventory across 10 stores
- Low stock alerts
- Transfer tracking between branches
- Batch and expiry management

### Stock Take (SA GMP Compliant)
Photo verification on every count. Audit-ready, always.

- Mobile app for counting
- Camera capture required (no faking counts)
- Variance tracking and approval workflow
- Automatic SAHPRA-ready reports

### Staff Management
Secure, simple, no passwords to remember or share.

- OTP login via email
- Role-based access (Owner → Manager → Assistant)
- Full audit trail
- Activity monitoring

### Daily Cashup
End-of-day reconciliation with owner approval.

- Till-by-till counting
- Manager submission
- Owner approval before banking
- Variance tracking

### Owner Dashboard
See everything, approve what matters, from anywhere.

- All 10 stores at a glance
- Pending approvals
- Sales trends
- Staff performance
