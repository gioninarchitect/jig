Two things we fixed on your POS

A quick, plain-English note on what was going wrong and what's better now — 10 June 2026.

Hi Ray — you flagged two things that were making the till frustrating. Both are now fixed and live on your screen. Here's what they were and what changes for you.

1. The day wouldn't close cleanly Fixed

What was happening: if a shift wasn't opened in the morning, the till would quietly keep adding the new day's sales onto yesterday's still-open shift. That's why the day-end totals looked wrong and a shift seemed to "run into the next day".

What's better now: the till will not let a sale go through if there's no shift open for today. If you try, it pops up and asks you to open today's shift first — so each day's takings stay on their own day.

2. The product tab jumped back to "All" Fixed

What was happening: every 30 seconds the till refreshes its product list in the background. That refresh was snapping your selected tab (e.g. Bio Sculpture or a flower category) back to "All", so you'd lose your place.

What's better now: the tab you've selected stays put through the refresh and after you add an item to the basket. It only changes when you tap a different tab yourself.

Your daily rhythm — to keep it clean

  1. Start of day: open today's shift before the first sale (the till will remind you if you forget).
  2. During the day: ring sales as normal — your selected product tab now stays where you put it.
  3. End of day: close the shift and run the cash-up before you leave, so the day's takings are sealed to that day.
  4. If the cash is more than R50 out: it will ask for a manager code. If that ever blocks you, call it in — we're sorting a cleaner way for that one.

To pull the updates onto the till straight away, tap reload — or we can run the quick update on the POSBANK for you. Anything still feels off, send a screen recording like you did and we'll jump on it.
