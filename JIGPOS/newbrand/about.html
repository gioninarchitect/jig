About Origin

Premium Cannabis Care by ILCO Farming (Pty) Ltd

Our Story

Origin by ILCO Farming was built on a foundation of agricultural expertise and a genuine passion for plant-based wellness. With deep roots in South Africa's farming sector, ILCO Farming (Pty) Ltd brings decades of cultivation know-how to the cannabis space.

Our mission is simple: make premium, SAHPRA-compliant medical cannabis accessible and affordable to all South Africans. Every product we offer is carefully curated, lab-tested, and sourced with the same standards that define our agricultural heritage.

Today, Origin serves patients from our Potchefstroom collection point and online store, delivering quality medical cannabis nationwide. Every product carries our commitment from day one: quality, care, and accessibility.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, affordable, and empowering medical cannabis care to South Africans through SAHPRA-compliant Section 21 operations, backed by the agricultural expertise of ILCO Farming.

We are committed to:

  • Making medical cannabis accessible to all who need it
  • Full SAHPRA compliance and Section 21 regulatory standards
  • Affordable pricing without compromising on quality
  • Educating patients and communities about medical cannabis
  • Empowering individuals to take control of their health naturally
