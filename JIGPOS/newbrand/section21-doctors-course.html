Built For Governance

This is not cannabis product advertising and does not promote unregistered medicines, therapeutic outcomes, strains, discounts, or lifestyle use. It is a controlled onboarding route for doctors operating in a regulated medical access workflow.

CMO-Controlled Standard

Credentialing, clinical independence, critical safety failures, QA review, and final activation are governed by the Chief Medical Officer or delegate.

Regulatory Alignment

The curriculum is aligned to Section 21 access principles, SAHPRA workflow expectations, informed consent, recordkeeping, confidentiality, advertising restrictions, and escalation duties.

Clinical First

Doctors learn to preserve independent clinical judgement, document rationale, manage risk, and reject retail or commercial pressure.

Regional Practitioner Route

Advanced candidates can move into regional QA, file review, branch escalation, remediation, and peer-support responsibilities.

CPD Readiness

The course is structured for CPD/CEU submission, but must not be advertised as accredited until an HPCSA-recognised accreditor approves the activity and CEU allocation.

Estimated Contact Time

Approximately 6.0 to 6.5 hours including 339 minutes of module learning, final MCQ assessment, and course evaluation.

Proposed CEU Request

Proposed for accreditor review: 5 Clinical CEUs plus 1 Ethics / Human Rights / Health Law CEU. Final allocation is subject to accreditor approval.

Measurable Outcome

Doctors must complete all modules and pass a formal MCQ assessment with a minimum score of 75% before a completion certificate is generated.

Evidence Pack

The course includes a governance pack, assessment, certificate template, evaluation questions, and CPD accreditation readiness pack.

Curriculum

Twelve modules give doctors enough context to learn the workflow, complete two practical application hours, and pass the multiple-choice assessment with a 75% minimum score.

01 Regulatory Framework

Section 21 purpose, named-patient applications, SAHPRA decisions, and pathway limits.

02 Clinical Independence

Patient selection, risk screening, refusal duties, and commercial-pressure controls.

03 Informed Consent

Plain-language consent, costs, risks, alternatives, uncertainty, and patient responsibilities.

04 Documentation

Audit-ready consultation records, clinical rationale, consent notes, and follow-up plans.

05 SAHPRA Workflow

Application fields, supporting evidence, requests for information, and outcome tracking.

06 Product Boundaries

COA, batch traceability, authorisation alignment, substitution rules, and fulfilment holds.

07 Patient Follow-Up

Monitoring, tolerability, non-response, renewal decisions, and clinical reassessment.

08 Pharmacovigilance

Adverse events, misuse, diversion, medication errors, urgent escalation, and SAHPRA reporting timelines.

09 Ethics and Privacy

Confidentiality, data minimisation, conflicts, fee disclosure, telehealth boundaries, and professional communication.

10 Network Governance

Provider status, QA outcomes, remediation, suspension, course completion certificates, and CMO/delegate certification.

11 Advanced Clinical Case Practicum

One hour of complex case work covering chronic pain, psychiatric risk, polypharmacy, safety-sensitive work, and referral decisions.

12 CPD Portfolio, Audit, and Quality Practicum

One hour of CPD evidence, QA file review, remediation planning, reflection, evaluation, and audit readiness.

How It Works

The public site explains the offer. Doctors then authenticate and complete the controlled course experience.

1

Doctor registers or logs in with OTP

A one-time code is sent through the existing Origin OTP authentication endpoint.

2

Doctor completes all 12 modules

The course dashboard tracks module progress locally in the browser and keeps the route focused on professional learning outcomes.

3

Doctor writes the MCQ assessment

The assessment requires a 75% pass mark. Critical safety concerns remain subject to CMO or delegate review.

4

Completion certificate generated for sign-off

The branded certificate captures doctor name, HPCSA number, score, and date, and clearly states that final certification is pending CMO or delegate sign-off.
