The Problem
  • Monthly stocktakes only catch theft or errors after weeks of accumulation — by then it's impossible to trace who, when, or how
  • High-value products (flower, pre-rolls, concentrates) are the most at-risk for shrinkage and the least frequently counted
  • Staff have no daily accountability for stock accuracy — discrepancies are only discovered during the monthly scramble
  • There is no system-enforced link between stock counts and day-end cashup — staff can close without ever counting
The Solution: Three-Tier Stock Count

Origin now operates a tiered stocktake schedule. Higher-value products are counted more frequently. The system enforces compliance — you cannot close the day without counting.

Tier Categories Frequency Who
Tier A Flower, Pre-rolls, Concentrates (dabs, rosin, wax) DAILY at close Shift Lead
Tier B Edibles, Oils, Vapes WEEKLY (Monday AM) Branch Manager
Tier C All categories (incl. accessories, lifestyle, merch) MONTHLY (1st week) Full team
System Enforcement The POS system blocks day-end cashup submission until the daily Tier A count is completed. Staff cannot skip or bypass this. The owner and inventory manager see real-time compliance per branch.

Owner: What You Need to Know

This briefing covers what's changing, what you'll see differently, and what mandate your staff need from you.

What You'll See: Owner Dashboard
  • Stock Count Compliance card — shows every branch with red/green status for daily, weekly, and monthly counts. At a glance: is each branch counting?
  • Red = overdue. If a branch hasn't done today's high-value count, it shows red immediately. No waiting for monthly reports.
  • Shrinkage trends — the system tracks which products consistently come up short across multiple counts, flagging patterns that suggest theft or procedural gaps.
The Mandate You Need to Give

For this system to work, staff need to hear directly from you that:

  • Daily stock counts are mandatory, not optional. This is a condition of employment, not a suggestion. The system enforces it — they cannot close the day without it.
  • The third-party implementer has your full authority to train, enforce, and escalate non-compliance. Staff must cooperate with training and follow the SOPs.
  • Variance is not punishment — it's visibility. The goal is to find problems early, not to punish staff. But repeated unexplained variance will trigger investigation.
  • The old way is over. Manual counting, paper records, and "I'll do it later" are finished. The digital system is the single source of truth.
Recommended: Send a WhatsApp or Voice Note Record a short voice note to all branch managers saying: "We are implementing daily stock counts on all high-value products. This is non-negotiable. [Implementer name] has my full backing to train and enforce this. Please cooperate fully." This eliminates any "I didn't know" or "I thought it was optional."
ROI: Why This Pays for Itself
  • If a branch loses even 5g of flower per day undetected, that's 150g/month. At R100-R200/g retail, that's R15,000-R30,000/month shrinkage per branch.
  • Daily counting costs ~30 minutes of staff time. That's R50-R80 in labour cost to protect R15,000+ in stock.
  • Across 8 branches, even a 50% reduction in shrinkage = R60,000-R120,000/month saved.

Red Flags to Watch For
  • Consistently late submissions — If a branch always submits at 23:00 or later, they may be rushing or faking counts
  • Zero variance on every count — Suspicious. Real counts always have small variances. Zero every day suggests they're entering expected values instead of counting
  • Same product shrinking every day — Use the shrinkage trends view. If "Purple Haze Indoor Loose" loses 3g every single day, that's a pattern
  • Missing scale photos on flower — Loose flower requires a scale photo with OCR. If they're skipping photos, they may be guessing weights
Branch Staff: What's Changing

Starting now, every branch does a stock count of high-value products at the end of every day. Here's exactly what you need to do.

The Daily Count (Every Day, No Exceptions)
1
30 minutes before close: Open the Stocktake App After the last customer leaves, open the Stocktake App on your device.
2
Tap "New Session" → Select "Daily High-Value" This is the red button at the top of the create screen. It automatically loads only flower, pre-rolls, and concentrates — nothing else.
3
Count every item Loose flower: Weigh on the scale, take a photo of the scale reading, enter the weight.
Pre-rolls / Pre-packs: Count the units, enter the number.
Concentrates: Count units (jars, syringes, cartridges).
Use Batch Mode (list icon) for faster entry.
4
Add notes for any variance If the count doesn't match what the system expects, add a note: breakage, sample, packaging error, etc. Don't just submit without explaining.
5
Submit the count Tap Submit. This unlocks your day-end cashup. Without submitting the count, the POS will not let you close the day.
6
Complete your normal day-end cashup Go to POS → Day End as usual. It will now allow submission.
If You Try to Submit Cashup Without Counting: You will see a red lock screen that says "Daily Stock Count Required". You MUST complete the count first. There is no override, no bypass, no workaround. Do the count.
Common Mistakes to Avoid
  • Don't enter the expected number. Count physically. If the system says 50 and you count 47, enter 47. The variance is the whole point.
  • Don't estimate. "Looks like about 30" is not a count. Pick up, count, record.
  • Don't skip the scale photo for loose flower. The system uses OCR to verify your weight entry. No photo = the submission may be rejected.
  • Don't leave variance unexplained. Every variance needs a note. "Don't know" is not acceptable — investigate first.
  • Don't wait until midnight. Start the count 30 minutes before close, not at the last minute when you're trying to leave.
FAQs
  • "Why do we need to count every day?"
    Because monthly counting means 30 days of undetected loss. Daily counting catches problems within 24 hours. A 5g daily loss becomes visible immediately instead of accumulating to 150g.
  • "It takes too long."
    The daily count only covers flower, pre-rolls, and concentrates — not the full product range. Using Batch Mode, most branches finish in 15-20 minutes.
  • "What if there's a variance but I didn't do anything wrong?"
    That's exactly why we count — to find the real cause. Breakage, packaging errors, incorrect receiving, and system bugs all cause variance. The note you add helps investigation. You are not automatically blamed for variance.
  • "Can the manager override the cashup block?"
    No. The system requires the count to be submitted. There is no manual override. This protects you as much as it protects the business — it proves you counted.
  • "What about weekends and public holidays?"
    If the branch trades, the count must be done. Every trading day. If you're closed, no count is needed.
Rollout Timeline
1
Week 1: Training & Dry Run All staff briefed (this document). Practice daily counts WITHOUT the cashup gate enabled. Build the habit. Identify issues.
2
Week 2: Soft Enforcement Daily counts expected but cashup gate shows a warning (not a block). Inventory manager reviews all submissions. Feedback to branches on accuracy.
3
Week 3: Full Enforcement Cashup gate fully active — no count = no cashup. All branches must comply. Compliance dashboard monitored daily by inventory manager and owner.
4
Month 2+: Steady State Daily counts are routine. Weekly shrinkage reviews. Monthly full stocktakes continue. Focus shifts from compliance to variance reduction.
