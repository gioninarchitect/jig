Section 1 · Cashier

Getting Started

How to switch on and sign in to the till.

1 The till starts itself

When the POSBANK is switched on, the Origin POS opens automatically, full-screen. If it ever closes, it reopens by itself within a second.

2 Log in

  • Type your email address using the on-screen keyboard.
  • Enter your 6-digit code (PIN or the OTP sent to email).
  • Tap DONE.
Tip: The keyboard has a full number row and a numpad for codes. The space bar is the wide key at the bottom.

3 Open the till for the day

Top-right shows the till status. If it says "Shift Closed," tap it → enter the opening cash float (or 0 if none) → the till is now Till Open and ready to sell.

Good to know: Logging out, reloading, or the screen restarting does NOT close the day. Only the Day End cash-up closes the till.

Section 2 · Cashier

Ringing Up Sales

Adding products to the customer's order.

1 Find the product

  • Use the two groups at the top: Cannabis and Wellness & Pharmacy.
  • Tap a category (Teas, Mushrooms, Skincare, Nail Gel, etc.) or filter by brand.
  • Or type in Search — by name, brand or SKU.

2 Add a normal product

Tap the product card — it drops into the cart on the left. Tap again to add another.

3 Sell tea by the gram

Teas are loose and sold per gram. Tap a tea (it shows e.g. R2 /g) → a numpad opens → type the grams (or tap 10g / 25g / 50g / 100g) → see the total → Add to Cart.

Tea prices: most teas R2/g · Ginger & Rooibos R1/g · Blue Lotus R6/g.

4 Change quantities in the cart

  • Use − / + or the quick buttons +1 +2 +5 +10 on each cart line.
  • Tap × to remove a line, or Clear Cart to start over.
VAT: The till adds 15% VAT automatically at checkout — you don't add it yourself.

Section 3 · Cashier

Taking Payment

Closing the sale and giving the customer their slip.

1 Go to checkout

Tap Checkout at the bottom of the cart. The Process Payment screen shows the Amount Due.

2 Choose the payment type

  • Cash — type the amount received on the numpad; the screen shows the change to give.
  • Card (Speedpoint) — swipe on the card machine, then type the Speedpoint reference.
  • Manual EFT — for a bank transfer; capture the reference.

2 Slip & drawer (automatic)

  • The receipt prints automatically to the printer — no pop-up, no PDF.
  • On a cash sale, the cash drawer pops open automatically. (Card/EFT do not open the drawer — no cash changes hands.)

3 Email the invoice (optional)

On the "Sale Complete" screen, type the customer's email and tap Email Receipt to send them a copy.

That's a complete sale. Tap New Sale / Done to serve the next customer.

Section 4 · Cashier + Manager

Fixing Mistakes — Void & Refund

Before payment you can just edit the cart. After a sale is paid, you need a manager.

1 Before paying

Just use −, × or Clear Cart — no approval needed.

2 After paying — open Transactions

Tap the gold 🧾 Sales button (top of the header) → find the slip (it's at the top, or search the slip number) → tap it.

3 Void or Refund

  • Void — for a sale rung up wrong / cancelled. Cancels it, puts stock back, removes it from takings.
  • Refund — for a customer return. Tick the items (all, or just some) to refund.
  • Type a short reason/note, then a manager/admin PIN to approve.
Manager PIN required. A cashier cannot void or refund alone — an owner/admin PIN must approve every one. Each is logged with who approved it.
After approval the slip shows a red VOIDED or orange REFUNDED badge, and the day's totals update automatically.

Section 5 · Cashier + Manager

Transactions List

A running record of the day's sales — anyone can view it.

1 Open it

Tap the gold 🧾 Sales button. You'll see Today's Transactions with the live net total at the top.

2 Read a row

Each row shows the slip number, time, item count, payment type (CASH / CARD / EFT) and amount, with a status badge: COMPLETED, VOIDED or REFUNDED.

3 Search

Type a slip number in the search box to jump to one sale. Tap a sale to see its items and (with a manager PIN) Void or Refund it.

The total is net. Voided/refunded sales are excluded from the total and shown separately, so the figure is the real takings.

Section 6 · Manager

Changing a Price

Prices can be corrected on the spot — but only an admin can approve it.

1 Tap the pencil

Every product card has a small gold pencil (top-left). Tap it to open Edit Product.

2 Enter the new price

Type the new price (for teas this is the per-gram rate). Stock is not edited here — that comes in through receiving/orders.

3 Approve

Enter an owner/admin PIN → Save · Admin Approve. The card updates instantly and shows "Approved by [name]."

Nothing changes without the PIN. The cashier's own PIN will not work — every price change is logged.

Section 7 · Manager

Day End & Cash-Up

Closing the trading day, counting the drawer, and sending the report.

1 Start day end

Tap the gold cash-register button (or the Till Open status). The cash-up screen opens.

2 Count the cash

Count the physical cash and type the quantity of each note & coin. The screen totals it and compares to the Expected Cash.

3 Check the variance

  • Within ±R50 → green, you can close.
  • Over R50 → a note + manager/admin PIN is required to close (over/short must be explained).

4 Confirm & report

  • You'll be asked "Close today's session?" — confirm only at true day end.
  • The Z-Report appears: Cash / Card / EFT breakdown, net sales, variance.
  • It auto-emails the PDF + CSV to the owner & head office, and you can Download PDF / CSV.
  • Tap Back to POS — you stay logged in for the next day.
Only Day End closes the till. Don't run it unless you're closing for the day.

Section 8 · Reference

Golden Rules & Troubleshooting

Keep these in mind — and how to fix the small stuff.

★ Golden rules

  • Every sale goes through the till — never on paper (VAT must be captured).
  • Voids & refunds always need a manager PIN + a reason.
  • Count the drawer honestly at day end — explain any variance.
  • Only run Day End when closing for the day.
