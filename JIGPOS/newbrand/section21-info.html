What is Section 21?

Section 21 of the Medicines and Related Substances Act allows for the legal use of unregistered medicines, including medical cannabis, when prescribed by a licensed healthcare provider for specific medical conditions.

How to Access Medical Cannabis

1Consult a Healthcare Provider

Schedule an appointment with a licensed doctor who can prescribe medical cannabis. They will:

  • Evaluate your medical condition
  • Determine if medical cannabis is appropriate for your treatment
  • Discuss potential benefits and risks
  • Create a treatment plan if suitable

If your doctor determines medical cannabis is suitable, they will provide:

  • Section 21 Authorization Letter - Valid for 180 days
  • Doctor's details and practice number
  • Specific conditions being treated

Important Requirements

Authorization Letter Must Include:

  • Doctor's full name and practice number
  • Patient's full name (matching your account)
  • Medical conditions being treated
  • Recommended cannabis products or cannabinoid ratios
  • Issue date (valid for 180 days)
  • Doctor's signature and practice stamp

Prescription Requirements:

  • Issued after Section 21 authorization approval
  • Specific products and quantities
  • Clear dosage instructions
  • Valid prescription period
  • Doctor's signature

Conditions Commonly Treated with Medical Cannabis

Medical cannabis may be prescribed for conditions including:

  • Chronic pain
  • Nausea and vomiting (especially from chemotherapy)
  • Multiple sclerosis
  • Epilepsy and seizure disorders
  • HIV/AIDS-related symptoms
  • Cancer-related symptoms
  • Post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD)
  • Inflammatory bowel disease
  • Palliative care

Note: This is not an exhaustive list. Your doctor will determine if medical cannabis is appropriate for your specific condition.

Legal Framework

Medicines and Related Substances Act (Act 101 of 1965)

Section 21 allows for:

  • Use of unregistered medicines for specific patients
  • Prescription by registered medical practitioners
  • Legal possession and use of prescribed medical cannabis

Renewal Process

Section 21 authorizations are valid for 180 days. To renew:

  1. Schedule a follow-up appointment with your doctor
  2. Doctor evaluates your treatment progress
  3. New authorization letter issued if treatment continues
  4. Upload new authorization through your dashboard
  5. Continue receiving medical cannabis with new prescription

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does verification take?

Authorization letter verification typically takes 24-48 hours during business days.

Can I use my authorization at multiple pickup points?

Your Section 21 authorization allows you to obtain medical cannabis from any licensed pickup point in South Africa.

What if my doctor is unfamiliar with Section 21?

We can provide your doctor with resources and information about the Section 21 process. Contact our support team.

Is my medical information kept confidential?

Yes. All medical information is encrypted, stored securely, and accessible only to authorized medical verification staff.

Can I order medical cannabis products without a prescription?

No. A valid prescription is required for each order of medical cannabis products, in addition to your Section 21 authorization.

What happens if my authorization expires?

You will need to obtain a new authorization letter from your doctor and submit it for verification before continuing to purchase medical cannabis products.
