Dashboard Overview & Navigation

Understanding the Inventory Hub layout, login, and sidebar menu

Logging In

The Inventory Manager dashboard uses OTP (One-Time Password) authentication. You can also use your 6-digit PIN for faster login.

  1. Navigate to inventory-manager-dashboard.html from the admin sidebar or direct URL
  2. Enter your email address (e.g., inventory@jig.cleva-ai.co.za)
  3. Click "Send Login Code" to receive your OTP via email
  4. Enter the 6-digit code in the OTP fields
  5. Alternative: Use your PIN login below the OTP form for quick access
Quick Tip
Your PIN is a permanent 6-digit code assigned by your admin. It's faster than OTP for daily use. Click the eye icon to reveal your PIN while typing.

Batch Management

Batch/lot tracking, cannabinoid profiles, QA approval workflow

What is a Batch?

A batch (or lot) is a specific production run of a product from a supplier. Each batch has:

  • Batch ID: Unique identifier for traceability
  • Product: Which product this batch belongs to
  • Supplier: Who supplied this batch
  • THC/CBD Profile: Cannabinoid percentages (for flower/concentrates)
  • Quantity: Number of units in this batch
  • Expiry Date: When the batch expires

QA Workflow

Every batch goes through a 4-stage quality assurance process:

  1. Pending QA: Batch has been received but not yet inspected. Appears in the Pending QA stat card
  2. QA Review: Inventory manager inspects the batch - checks weight, quality, cannabinoid profile, packaging integrity
  3. Approved: Batch passes QA. Stock becomes available for sale at branches. Approved
  4. Rejected: Batch fails QA. Flagged for return to supplier or disposal. Rejected

Creating a New Batch

  1. Click "Create Batch" button in the top right
  2. Select the product from the dropdown
  3. Select the supplier who provided this batch
  4. Enter the THC and CBD percentages from the lab report
  5. Enter the quantity received
  6. Set the expiry date
  7. Click Save - batch enters "Pending QA" status

Filtering Batches

Use the filter bar to find specific batches:

  • Status: Pending QA, Approved, Rejected, or Expired
  • Supplier: Filter by supplier name
  • Search: Type batch ID or product name
Important
Expired batches are flagged automatically. Products from expired batches must NOT be sold. The system blocks POS sales of expired batch items.

Purchase Orders

Creating, approving, and tracking purchase orders from suppliers

PO Lifecycle

Purchase orders follow this status flow:

Draft Submitted Approved Ordered Partially Received Received

Creating a Purchase Order

  1. Click "Create Purchase Order" button
  2. Select a supplier from the dropdown
  3. Add line items: Select products and enter quantities needed
  4. Review the total cost (unit price x quantity for each line)
  5. Set the expected delivery date
  6. Save as Draft (edit later) or Submit (send for approval)

Approving Purchase Orders

POs submitted by branch managers or assistants require approval from an inventory manager, admin, or owner.

  1. Filter by "Submitted" status to see POs awaiting approval
  2. Click on the PO to review line items and total
  3. Approve to allow ordering, or Reject with a reason
  4. Once approved, mark as "Ordered" when sent to supplier

Receiving Goods

When a delivery arrives:

  1. Find the PO and click "Receive"
  2. Enter the actual quantities received per line item
  3. If partial delivery, the PO moves to "Partially Received"
  4. When all items received, PO moves to "Received" and stock is automatically updated
Pro Tip
Use the Stock Receiving App (stock-receiving-app.html) on a tablet at the receiving dock for a streamlined goods-in workflow. It connects to the same PO system.

Stock Alert Thresholds

The system uses configurable thresholds (set in Settings) to generate alerts:

  • Out of Stock (0 units): Critical - Product cannot be sold
  • Low Stock (1-10 units): Warning - Needs reorder soon
  • Below Reorder Point (1-20 units): Reorder - Auto-reorder may trigger
  • Healthy (20+ units): Good - No action needed
How Branch Stock Works
Each product has a BranchInventory record per branch. When POS makes a sale, the branch's quantity decreases. The Stock Levels section shows a consolidated view across all branches so you can spot which branches need replenishment.

Auto Reorder Rules

Setting up automatic purchase orders when stock drops below thresholds

How Auto Reorder Works

When a product's stock falls below its Reorder Point, the system automatically creates a Purchase Order for the configured Reorder Quantity from the preferred supplier.

Example
Product "Divine Storm" has: Reorder Point = 50g, Reorder Qty = 200g, Supplier = Greendoor. When stock drops to 48g, the system auto-creates a PO for 200g from Greendoor.

Creating a Reorder Rule

  1. Click "Add Rule" button
  2. Select the product
  3. Set the Reorder Point (stock level that triggers the rule)
  4. Set the Reorder Quantity (how much to order)
  5. Select the preferred supplier
  6. Choose Auto-Order (creates PO automatically) or Manual Approval (notifies you to review first)
  7. Save the rule - it runs automatically on every stock change

Filtering Rules

  • Active Only: Rules currently monitoring stock
  • Inactive: Paused rules
  • Recently Triggered: Rules that recently created a PO
  • Auto-Order Enabled: Fully automatic rules
  • Manual Approval Required: Rules that notify but don't auto-create POs
Best Practice
Start with Manual Approval for expensive or slow-moving products, and Auto-Order for high-velocity items like popular flower strains and edibles.

Stock Take (PWA App)

Mobile stock counting with AI photo verification - scale reading and unit counting

What is the Stock Take App?

A Progressive Web App (PWA) designed for mobile use at each branch. Staff physically count products and photograph evidence. The app uses AI vision (Claude Haiku) to automatically read scale weights and count units from photos.

Stock Take Types

TypeScopeWhen to Use
Full CountAll products in the branchMonthly or quarterly full inventory audit
Cycle CountSpecific categories onlyWeekly rotating category counts
Spot CheckSelected productsRandom verification or discrepancy investigation

Photo Evidence & AI Reading

The app captures two types of photos:

Photo TypeUsed ForAI Does
Scale PhotoWeighable items (loose flower, concentrates)Reads the weight displayed on the digital scale (e.g., "142.5g")
Unit PhotoCountable items (pre-rolls, edibles, vapes)Counts the individual items visible in the photo
AI Accuracy
Scale reading: 95% confidence using Claude Haiku vision. Unit counting: 90% confidence. The AI result is compared to your manual entry - if they differ by more than 5% for weighable items, it's flagged for review.

Stock Take Workflow

  1. Open the app on your phone/tablet at the branch. Log in with email + PIN
  2. Start or resume a session: Select Full, Cycle, or Spot count type
  3. Filter by category: Use Flower, Edibles, Concentrates, Vapes, Accessories tabs
  4. For each product: Tap to expand, then enter the counted quantity
  5. Take a photo: For weighable items, photograph the scale. For countable items, photograph the units laid out
  6. AI verifies: The app sends the photo to Claude Vision API. It reads the weight or counts units and compares with your entry
  7. Review variances: Items with significant differences between expected and actual are highlighted
  8. Submit for approval: When all items are counted, submit the session
  9. Manager reviews: Branch manager or inventory manager approves the count

Variance Thresholds

Item TypeThresholdIf Exceeded
Countable (pre-rolls, edibles, etc.)0% (exact match required)Flagged as variance - requires investigation
Weighable (loose flower, concentrates)5% toleranceFlagged if variance exceeds 5% of expected weight
Cost
AI photo processing costs approximately R0.038 per photo (Claude Haiku). For a full stock take of 150 products, that's about R5.70 per session. Monthly cost across all 8 branches: approximately R23 per month.

MDC Control Panel

Master Digital Catalogue - the 4-stage product lifecycle pipeline

What is the MDC?

The Master Digital Catalogue (MDC) is the central product database for the entire JIG Craft Cannabis network. As Inventory Manager, you are the MDC Custodian - you control which products go live across all 8 branches.

The 4-Stage Pipeline

Products move through four stages on a Kanban-style board:

  1. Pending: New product added to system. Needs initial review of name, description, pricing, images, and category classification
  2. QA Review: Product undergoes quality check. Verify supplier info, batch data, cannabinoid profile, and compliance tags (Section 21 vs Lifestyle)
  3. Approved: Product passed QA. Now ready for branch stock allocation. Use the Branch Allocator to distribute stock to specific branches
  4. Live: Product is active on the POS network and available for sale at allocated branches

MDC Tools

ToolPurposeWhen to Use
Pricing ControlSet retail, wholesale, and cost prices for products across the networkWhen setting up new products or adjusting pricing
Bulk TaggerTag multiple products with track classification (Section 21, Lifestyle, or Both)During product onboarding or reclassification
Branch AllocatorDistribute stock quantities from approved products to specific branchesAfter QA approval, before going live
MDC ReportGenerate a report of the full catalogue statusMonthly audits, management reviews

Using the Branch Allocator

  1. Click "Branch Allocator" in the tools bar (or click allocate on an approved product card)
  2. Select the product to allocate
  3. See the total available stock and current branch distribution
  4. Enter quantities for each of the 8 branches
  5. The total allocated must not exceed available stock
  6. Click Save Allocation - stock is distributed and product moves to Live
Critical Rule
A product CANNOT go Live without branch stock allocation. The MDC enforces this: Approved products stay in the Approved column until you allocate stock to at least one branch.

Section 21 Compliance

South African law requires Section 21 authorization for medical cannabis products. The compliance panel shows:

  • Pending Approvals: Users who have submitted Section 21 prescriptions awaiting verification
  • Approved Users: Users with verified Section 21 authorization who can purchase medical products
Legal Requirement
Never approve a Section 21 user without verifying their prescription document. Invalid approvals expose the business to legal liability. When in doubt, escalate to the owner or admin.
