Ormonde Go-Live — UAT Checklist

User Acceptance Testing for the Stock Take App. Complete all items before going live at Ormonde branch. Each role must be tested independently to confirm correct access levels.

  • All staff PINs created and tested
  • Tablet bookmarked to stocktake-app.html
  • Current physical stock sheet collected from branch — this is the first step before going digital. The physical count becomes the baseline.
  • WiFi connectivity confirmed at branch location
  • Test login with each staff PIN to verify access
Important: Do not proceed with digital stock take until the physical stock sheet has been collected and recorded. This is the baseline reference for all variance calculations.
Owner
  • Login with owner PIN
  • Create new stock take session (Full Count)
  • View all product categories: Flower, Edibles, Concentrates, Vapes, Accessories
  • Filter by grow method (Indoor / Greendoor) and type (Pre Rolls / Pre Packs / Loose)
  • Count items: enter weight, take scale photo
  • Verify variance displays correctly against expected quantity
  • Export stock take to CSV
  • Submit stock take for review
Receive Stock workflow — tested from the same page, no logout required.
  • Switch to Receive Stock tab (same page, no logout)
  • Search and add products to receive
  • Enter quantities and complete receiving
Staff Manager
  • Login with staff manager PIN
  • Create or resume a stock take session
  • Full counting workflow — search, filter, enter weight, photo, save
  • Export stock take to CSV
  • Submit completed stock take
  • Switch to Receive Stock tab
  • Search, add products, enter quantities, complete receiving
Staff
  • Login with staff PIN
  • Resume an existing stock take session
  • Count assigned items — enter weight values
  • Take photos of scale readings
  • Confirm: Submit button is disabled or hidden (manager approval required)
Access control verification: Staff members should be able to count and record data, but must not have the ability to submit or finalise a stock take. This requires manager or owner approval.

Training Guide

Step-by-step instructions for using the Stock Take App. This guide covers login, counting, receiving stock, and best practices for accurate inventory management.

  • 1 Open the app on the tablet browser: app.jig.cleva-ai.co.za/stocktake-app.html
  • 2 Enter your email address
  • 3 Enter your 6-digit PIN
  • 4 Tap "Login with PIN"
Tip: Bookmark the app URL on the tablet home screen for quick access. Your PIN is set up by the branch manager or owner — contact them if you have trouble logging in.
  • 1 Select or create a stock take session
  • 2 Use the search bar or category filters to find products
  • 3 Tap a product to expand it
  • 4 Take a photo of the scale reading (required)
  • 5 Enter the weight shown on the scale
  • 6 Variance auto-calculates against the expected quantity
  • 7 Add notes if needed (e.g. damaged packaging, spillage)
  • 8 Save the item
  • 9 Repeat for all products in the session
  • 10 When complete, tap Submit
Scale photo is mandatory. Every weight entry must be accompanied by a photograph of the scale display. This provides an auditable record and protects both staff and the business.
  • 1 Tap the "Receive Stock" tab at the top of the page
  • 2 Search for products being received
  • 3 Tap + to add each product to the receiving list
  • 4 Enter the quantity received (in grams)
  • 5 Tap "Complete" when all items have been entered
  • 6 Tap the "Stock Take" tab to return to counting
No logout required. The Stock Take and Receive Stock tabs are on the same page. Switch between them freely during your session.
  • Always photograph the scale before entering weight. This is your proof of accuracy and protects you during audits.
  • Check variance after each entry. Large variances will be flagged for management review. If you spot an error, re-weigh and update before saving.
  • Work through products systematically. Use category filters (Flower, Edibles, Concentrates, Vapes, Accessories) to count one section at a time.
  • Export to CSV at any time. Use this as a backup or to share partial results with management before submitting.
  • Use the grow method filter (Indoor / Greendoor) and type filter (Pre Rolls / Pre Packs / Loose) to narrow down large product lists.
  • Add notes for anything unusual. Damaged packaging, partial containers, or products nearing expiry should all be documented in the notes field.

Branch Rollout Plan

Standardised process for rolling out the Stock Take App to each branch. Ormonde is the pilot location. Follow these steps in order for each subsequent branch.

  • Get the physical stock count from each branch first
  • This becomes the baseline for the digital system
  • Record format: product name, current quantity, unit (grams)
This is non-negotiable. Every branch must provide their current physical stock sheet before the app is introduced. Without a verified baseline, variance calculations are meaningless.
  • All staff already have PINs — no email setup needed
  • Tablets are already in store
  • Bookmark app.jig.cleva-ai.co.za/stocktake-app.html on each tablet
Verify each PIN works by having every staff member log in during setup. This avoids delays on the day of the first count.
  • 1 Open Chrome on the tablet
  • 2 Navigate to app.jig.cleva-ai.co.za/stocktake-app.html
  • 3 Add to Home Screen (optional, for quick access)
  • 4 Test login with one staff PIN to confirm connectivity
Adding to Home Screen: In Chrome, tap the three-dot menu, then select "Add to Home screen". This creates an app-like icon on the tablet for faster access.
  • 1 Manager or owner must be present for the first stock take
  • 2 Staff completes a full count using the digital app
  • 3 Compare results against the physical stock sheet baseline
  • 4 Resolve any major variances — re-weigh if necessary
  • 5 This validates that the system is producing accurate results
Do not skip the supervised count. The first digital count must be verified against the physical baseline. Only proceed to independent counts once accuracy is confirmed.
  • Staff performs daily/weekly counts independently using the app
  • Manager reviews and approves all submissions
  • Stock receiving is done through the app (Receive Stock tab)
  • Physical stock sheets are retired
Support: If any issues arise after going live, contact the branch manager or system administrator. The app URL remains app.jig.cleva-ai.co.za/stocktake-app.html.
