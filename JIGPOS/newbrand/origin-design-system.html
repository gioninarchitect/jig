07 · Voice & do / don't

Plain, warm, precise. Speak to a busy store owner, not a developer.

Do

  • Lead with the number that matters, then explain.
  • Use the Origin Icon Series for every glyph.
  • Say "Saved" / "Reconciled" — short, human, definite.
  • Flag regulated claims as VERIFY until signed off.

Don't

  • No emoji. Ever. (🌿 ✓ ✉ → use the icon set.)
  • No second accent colour competing with gold.
  • No jargon, no "please try again later".
  • No unverified medical/CBD claims in shipped copy.

08 · Sub-brand architecture

One design system, several surfaces — all share the palette, type and icons.
Origin Retail — the POS, stock sheet, takings reports, day-end. Operational, dense, number-first.

Origin Botanica — the farm-to-skin cosmetic range. Same gold, warmer/botanical accents, lifestyle photography.

Origin Medical / Patient Portal — Section 21, prescriptions, pharmacist review. Clinical, calm, compliance-led.

TnT-ZA (farm platform) — track & trace, batches, compliance. Same tokens, JetBrains Mono-forward for data density.
