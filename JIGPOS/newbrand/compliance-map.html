Regulatory Authorities of South Africa

STATUTE

SAHPRA

Medicines & Related Substances Act 101/1965

SA Health Products Regulatory Authority. Mandate: registers medicines & health products; licenses manufacturers & distributors (s22C); conducts GMP inspections & issues GMP certificates; administers Section 21 named-patient access to unregistered medicines; controls scheduling (s22A).
Origin/ILCO touchpoints: 22C licence, GMP cert (submitted 21 Jan 2026), Section 21 pathway. ⚠ Licence class & cannabis scheduling specifics to be specialist-confirmed.

STATUTE

SAPC — Pharmacy Council

Pharmacy Act 53/1974 · GPP rules (GNR)

SA Pharmacy Council. Statutory body that registers pharmacists, pharmacy support personnel & pharmacies, sets and enforces Good Pharmacy Practice (GPP), and runs CPD. Owns the rules for dispensing, recording and transport of medicines (GPP 2.7.5).
Origin touchpoints: pharmacy registration / Y-number, Responsible Pharmacist, dispensing & last-mile delivery compliance.

STATUTE

HPCSA

Health Professions Act 56/1974

Health Professions Council of SA. Registers and governs medical practitioners — the prescribers in the Section 21 / clinical-access pathway and the medical-cannabis prescription flow.

Grounding rule: Authority mandates above are grounded to their governing Acts (Medicines Act 101/1965, Pharmacy Act 53/1974, Health Professions Act 56/1974) and are factual at the framework level. Items still marked ⚠ verify (Section 21 specifics, per-product scheduling, ILCO's exact licence class & conditions) must be confirmed by a SA medicines-law specialist before being asserted in any shipped or customer-facing copy. EU GMP references are grounded in the official EudraLex Volume 4 source. This map is the internal source-of-truth registry, not legal advice.
