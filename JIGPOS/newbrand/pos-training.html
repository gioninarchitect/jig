Starting Your Shift

Log In to the System
Go to the branch login page. Enter your staff email (e.g., potchefstroom.assistant@cleva-ai.co.za) and click "Send Login Code". You'll receive a 6-digit OTP code.
Tip: Keep your phone nearby - OTP codes expire after 5 minutes.
Enter OTP Code
Enter the 6-digit code in the verification boxes. The system will automatically verify when all 6 digits are entered.
Select Your Till
Choose the till terminal assigned to you (Till 1, Till 2, etc.). Only one person can operate a till at a time.
Open Your Till
Count the cash float in the drawer. The standard float is R500. Enter the counted amount and click "OPEN TILL" to begin.
Important: If the float doesn't match R500, report to your manager BEFORE opening the till.

Processing Sales

Adding Products to Cart
Browse products on the right side of the screen. Click a product to add it to the cart (on the left). Use the + and - buttons to adjust quantities.
Layout: Cart is always on the LEFT, Products on the RIGHT (Origin STYLE).
Review the Cart
Verify all items and quantities. The total updates automatically. Remove items by clicking the trash icon.
Select Payment Method
Choose how the customer wants to pay:

Cash

  • Enter amount received
  • System calculates change
  • Count change back to customer
  • Drawer opens automatically

Card (Speedpoint)

  • Enter amount on Speedpoint device
  • Customer taps/inserts card
  • Wait for "APPROVED"
  • Enter reference in POS

EFT

  • Show banking details
  • Customer does transfer
  • Verify Proof of Payment
  • Enter EFT reference
Complete the Sale
Click the appropriate "COMPLETE SALE" button. Receipt prints automatically. Hand receipt to customer.

Card Payment - Speedpoint Process

Important: Card machines are NOT integrated. You must manually process on Speedpoint AND enter the reference in POS.
Process on Speedpoint Device
Enter the exact sale amount on the Speedpoint terminal. Customer presents their card (tap, insert, or swipe).
Wait for Bank Approval
Screen shows "PROCESSING..." then "APPROVED" or "DECLINED". If declined, ask customer for alternative payment.
Get the Reference Number
Speedpoint prints a receipt with a reference number (e.g., SP-20260204-12345). This is CRITICAL for reconciliation.
Enter Reference in POS
In the POS Card payment section, enter the Speedpoint reference number. Add notes if needed (e.g., "Visa Credit", customer name).
Tip: Double-check the reference before completing. This links the POS sale to the bank transaction.
Complete Card Sale
Click "COMPLETE CARD SALE". Give customer both receipts (Speedpoint + POS).

Common Scenarios

Customer Wants to Return an Item
  1. Ask for receipt and verify purchase date (within 7 days)
  2. Call manager for refund authorization
  3. Manager processes refund from their dashboard
  4. Assistants cannot process refunds directly
Card Payment Declined
  1. Politely inform customer the card was declined
  2. Offer to try again or ask for alternative payment
  3. Never discuss potential reasons in front of other customers
  4. If customer leaves, void the POS transaction
Product Out of Stock
  1. Check with manager if more stock is in back room
  2. Offer similar alternative products
  3. If customer wants to wait, take their contact for restock notification
  4. Never sell display items without manager approval

Branch Manager

Responsible for daily operations, staff supervision, cashups, stock management, and ensuring compliance with all procedures.

All Assistant Permissions Process Refunds Daily Cashup Approve Stock Takes Manage Staff View Reports

Daily Cashup Process

Daily cashup can only begin after ALL tills have been closed by their operators.
Verify All Tills Closed
Check the dashboard to ensure all tills show "Closed" status. Contact any operator with an open till.
Access Cashup Module
Go to Manager Dashboard > End of Day > Start Cashup.
Review Till Summaries
System shows aggregated totals from all tills: Cash, Card, EFT breakdowns.
Physical Cash Count
Combine cash from all tills. Count total. Separate R500 float for next day.
Reconcile Speedpoint
Run end-of-day batch on Speedpoint device. Compare batch total to POS card total.
Tip: Keep Speedpoint batch slip for records - required for variance investigation.
Enter All Counted Amounts
Enter actual counted/verified amounts for each payment method.
Document Variances
Add notes explaining any discrepancies. All variances must be documented.
Variances over R100 may trigger owner review before approval.
Submit for Owner Approval
Click "SUBMIT CASHUP" to send to owner/HQ for final approval.

Stock Take Management

Schedule Stock Take
Create a stock take session from Manager Dashboard > Stock Take > New Session. Choose Full, Cycle, or Spot check.
Assign Staff
Assign assistants to count specific categories or areas. They use the Stock Take app.
Monitor Progress
Track completion in real-time. See which items are counted, pending, or flagged.
Review Variances
When staff submit, review all items with >5% variance. Check photos match counts.
SA GMP Compliance: Every count MUST have a photo. Reject any items without photos.
Approve or Request Recount
Approve valid counts. Request recount for suspicious variances. Add notes.
Finalize Session
Once approved, inventory updates to match counted quantities. SAHPRA report generates automatically.

Processing Refunds

Verify Original Sale
Look up the original transaction by sale number or date. Verify items being returned.
Check Return Policy
Returns must be within 7 days with receipt. Products must be unopened/unused.
Cannabis Products: Cannot be returned once sealed packaging is opened.
Process Refund
Select refund items and choose refund method (same as original payment preferred).
Add Refund Reason
Document the reason for refund. This is required for all refunds.
Complete Refund
Process refund. Cash refunds from till. Card refunds processed on Speedpoint.

User Management
