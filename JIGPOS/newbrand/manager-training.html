Section 1

Your Manager Tools

As manager you have everything the cashier has, plus stock control, approvals and day-end.

1 Log in

Sign in on the POS with your email + PIN. As a manager you'll see extra buttons in the header: 📦 Stock, 🧾 Sales and the gold cash-register (Day-End) button.

2 What only you can do

  • Open the Stock Sheet — add/edit/hide products & prices
  • Approve a void or refund (cashier needs your PIN)
  • Approve a price change (your PIN)
  • Approve a cash variance over R50 at day-end
  • Run Day-End cash-up and send the Z-report
Your PIN is the key. Nothing sensitive changes without a manager/admin PIN — and every action is logged with your name.

Section 2 · Most used

The Stock Sheet

Your control room for products, prices and stock. Open it from the 📦 Stock button, or origin.cleva-ai.co.za/stock-sheet.html.

1 Find a product

Products are grouped into category accordions — tap a category to open it. Use Search (name, SKU, brand) or the low-stock filter. Expand all / Collapse all at the top.

2 Edit a product

  • Change the price, size or stock quantity in the row, then tap Save — it reflects on the till immediately.
  • For teas the price is the per-gram rate.

2 Add a new product

Tap + Add Product → enter name, category, size, price, stock (and brand). It appears on the POS straight away, and stock deducts automatically on every sale.

3 Remove something you don't carry

Tap Hide on the row (or untick On POS → Save). It's removed from the till but kept in the records. Untick later to bring it back.

Power tools: CSV exports your whole stock list; % Bulk price adjusts every visible product at once (filter to a category first).
Golden rule: the shelf price tag must match the till. Prices are VAT-inclusive — what you type is what the customer pays.

Section 3

Approvals — Void, Refund & Price

The cashier starts these; you approve with your PIN. Every one is logged.

1 Void / Refund a sale

  • Open 🧾 Sales → find the slip → Void (rung up wrong) or Refund (return — full or selected items).
  • Enter a short reason and your manager PIN → it processes, stock returns, and the day's takings update.

2 Change a price on the floor

Tap the small gold pencil on a product card → enter the new price → your PIN → Save · Admin Approve. (Bulk/permanent changes are better done in the Stock Sheet.)

A cashier's PIN won't work for any of these — it must be a manager/admin PIN. That's the control.

Section 4

Day-End & Reporting

Closing the trading day, counting the drawer, sending the report.

1 Check the day first

Open 🧾 Sales to see the day's transactions — the net total (voids excluded) and any VOIDED/REFUNDED slips. Tap Shift Summary for the Cash / Card / EFT split.

2 Run Day-End

  • Tap the gold cash-register button → count the physical cash and enter each note/coin quantity.
  • The screen shows Expected vs Counted and the variance.
  • Within ±R50 → closes freely. Over R50 → add a note (you're the manager, so you can close it; a cashier would need your PIN).

3 Confirm & report

  • Confirm "Close today's session?" — only at true day end.
  • The Z-Report appears (Cash/Card/EFT, net sales, variance) and auto-emails the PDF + CSV to head office. You can also Download PDF / CSV.
  • Tap Back to POS — you stay logged in.
Only Day-End closes the till. Logging out, reloading or rebooting will NOT close the day.

Daily Checklist & Sign-off

A quick rhythm to keep the branch tight.

WhenDo
OpenLog in · confirm till shows "Till Open" · check float
During the dayApprove voids/refunds & price changes · keep prices/stock right in the Stock Sheet · watch low-stock
RestockAdd new products & set stock in the Stock Sheet as deliveries arrive
CloseCheck Sales/Shift Summary · run Day-End · count cash · confirm Z-report emailed

? If something looks wrong

  • Screen stuck/old → close the POS (it reopens); or run the update tool. Tell IT if it persists.
  • Sale won't complete → check the cart, retry; note the error message.
  • Slip didn't print → check paper is the right way up; reprint from the sale.
