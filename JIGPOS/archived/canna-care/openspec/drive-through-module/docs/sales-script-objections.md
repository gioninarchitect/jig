# FINAL TIPS FOR SALES CALLS

## DO's:
1. **Listen more than you talk** (60% listening, 40% talking)
2. **Use their name often** (builds rapport)
3. **Ask clarifying questions** (understand their real concerns)
4. **Use data, not feelings** (numbers don't lie)
5. **Create urgency** (competitors, market timing, exclusivity)
6. **Book next steps DURING the call** (don't leave without a follow-up date)
7. **Address objections directly** (don't dodge, confront head-on)
8. **Paint the future** (help them visualize R1M/month in revenue)

## DON'Ts:
1. **Don't bad-mouth competitors** (stay classy)
2. **Don't oversell** (let the numbers speak)
3. **Don't accept "I'll think about it"** (push for specific concerns)
4. **Don't discount too easily** (value your product)
5. **Don't skip the close** (ask for the sale!)
6. **Don't leave without next steps** (book the follow-up)
7. **Don't lie about features** (honesty builds trust)
8. **Don't forget to follow up** (80% of sales happen after the 5th follow-up)
