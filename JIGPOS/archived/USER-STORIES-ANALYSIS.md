## 👤 ROLE 1: LIFESTYLE MEMBER (Regular User)

### User Story 1: Browse & Purchase CBD Products
**As a** lifestyle member
**I want to** browse cannabis accessories and CBD wellness products
**So that** I can maintain my wellness routine and lifestyle choices

**Critical Features**:
- ✅ View product catalog with images, prices, descriptions
- ✅ Filter by category (grinders, papers, CBD oils, edibles, beverages)
- ✅ Search products by name or type
- ✅ Add products to cart
- ✅ View cart with running total
- ✅ Checkout with delivery address
- ✅ Select payment method (Cash on Delivery, Card, EFT)
- ⚠️ **MISSING**: Product reviews/ratings
- ⚠️ **MISSING**: Compare products side-by-side
- ⚠️ **MISSING**: Wishlist/Save for later

**Why These Matter**:
- **Reviews**: Build trust, help customers make informed decisions
- **Compare**: CBD products have varying strengths (5%, 10%, 20% CBD) - users need to compare
- **Wishlist**: Wellness is ongoing - users plan purchases over time

## 🚨 CRITICAL MISSING FEATURES (HIGH PRIORITY)

### 1. Barcode/SKU System
**Current**: Manual product search by name
**Needed**: Barcode scanning, SKU lookup
**Impact**: Checkout speed 10x slower, human error
**Priority**: **CRITICAL** for Bean & Bud café (busy mornings)

### 2. Till Management (Cash Reconciliation)
**Current**: No shift management
**Needed**: Opening float, closing count, cash over/short
**Impact**: Cannot track cash, theft risk, accounting nightmare
**Priority**: **CRITICAL** for cash business

### 3. Stock Receiving & Purchase Orders
**Current**: Manual inventory updates
**Needed**: Receive stock shipments, update inventory automatically
**Impact**: Inventory inaccuracy, stockouts
**Priority**: **HIGH** for operations

### 4. Customer Loyalty Lookup at POS
**Current**: Customers can't redeem points in-store
**Needed**: Scan wellness card, apply points, earn points
**Impact**: Loyalty program useless in physical store
**Priority**: **HIGH** for customer retention

### 5. Returns & Refunds
**Current**: Cannot process returns
**Needed**: Return items, refund to original payment method
**Impact**: Legal non-compliance (CPA), customer dissatisfaction
**Priority**: **HIGH** for compliance

### 6. Section 21 Application Workflow
**Current**: Documents uploaded, no approval process
**Needed**: Review queue, approve/reject, expiry tracking
**Impact**: Legal risk, cannot actually sell medical cannabis
**Priority**: **CRITICAL** for legal compliance

### 7. Reporting & Analytics
**Current**: No reports
**Needed**: Sales reports, inventory reports, staff performance
**Impact**: Flying blind, cannot make business decisions
**Priority**: **HIGH** for business intelligence

### 8. Product Variants
**Current**: Each CBD strength is separate product
**Needed**: One product with variant dropdown (5%, 10%, 20%)
**Impact**: Messy catalog, poor UX, inventory confusion
**Priority**: **MEDIUM** for UX
