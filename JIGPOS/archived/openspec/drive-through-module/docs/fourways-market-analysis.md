## Key Success Factors for Fourways

### Advantages
1. **Affluent Market**: High disposable income in catchment area
2. **24/7 Unique Offering**: No competitors with this model
3. **High Traffic Area**: 50,000+ daily mall visitors
4. **Growing Market**: Cannabis acceptance increasing (Fourways Mall +24% grocery growth)
5. **Vehicle-Friendly**: Major highways (N1, William Nicol Drive)
6. **Premium Location**: "Champagne North" - SA's wealthiest area

### Challenges
1. **Market Education**: 24/7 drive-through cannabis is new concept
2. **Section 21 Complexity**: Medical cannabis requires prescriptions
3. **Competition**: Existing dispensaries in Sandton/Rivonia
4. **Regulatory Risk**: Cannabis laws evolving

### Mitigation Strategies
1. **Marketing**: Heavy local marketing (mall partnerships, social media)
2. **Section 21 Support**: Help customers obtain prescriptions
3. **Premium Service**: Fast, discreet, professional (justify 24/7 convenience)
4. **Product Mix**: Balance Section 21 + CBD + accessories (reduce medical-only dependency)
