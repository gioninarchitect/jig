Short Courses

SHORT COURSES

Skills Programmes

SKILLS PROGRAMMES

Occupationally based and when completed will carry credits towards a qualification registered in terms of the National Qualifications Framework.

Our courses provide prospective students with an opportunity to gain attitudes, skills and knowledge in the Cannabis Sector Management, Business Administration and Horticulture domains.

Our Careers Service empowers students to take ownership of and make informed decisions about their futures through a broad range of advisory services and networking opportunities. Our Curriculum covers the skills, knowledge and best practices for succeeding in the cannabis industry, including a strong focus on regulatory compliance.

Gain the specialized skills and knowledge to work in the growing cannabis sector with our Cannabis Education courses. As the industry is predicted to reach a peak by 2023, trained professionals are in high demand. Our courses are taught by practicing industry specialists through their experiences, research and Grow Journals. These courses provide the flexibility to work while building skills and knowledge to further your career.
