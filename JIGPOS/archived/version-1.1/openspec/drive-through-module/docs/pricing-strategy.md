# Drive-Through Module - Tiered Pricing Strategy

**Module**: 24/7 Medical Cannabis Drive-Through
**Company**: CBD Wellness 24
**Date**: November 2024

---

## Pricing Structure Overview

### Tiered Pricing Model

| Location Type | Monthly Price | Discount | Use Case |
|--------------|---------------|----------|----------|
| **HQ (Fourways)** | **R3,999/month** | Base Price | Pilot site, full development cost, premium support |
| **Branch Location** | **R1,999/month** | 50% off | Additional stores after HQ success |
| **Enterprise (3+ Locations)** | **R1,499/month per location** | 62% off | Multi-branch rollout discount |

---

## Pricing Rationale

### HQ Pricing: R3,999/month (Fourways)

**Why Premium Pricing for HQ?**

1. **Development Cost Recovery**:
   - Full custom development and integration
   - Pilot site testing and refinement
   - 24/7 monitoring and support during launch
   - On-site training and setup assistance

2. **Highest ROI Location**:
   - Fourways projected revenue: R787,500 - R1,050,000/month
   - Module cost: R3,999/month
   - **ROI: 197x - 263x return**
   - Break-even: ~20 orders/day @ R150 basket

3. **Premium Features**:
   - Priority support (24/7 technical assistance)
   - Custom feature requests
   - Quarterly performance reviews
   - Access to beta features first

4. **Market Justification**:
   - Fourways affluent market can bear premium pricing
   - R3,999 is 0.38% - 0.51% of projected monthly revenue
   - Minimal impact on profitability

### Branch Pricing: R1,999/month (50% Discount)

**Why 50% Discount for Additional Branches?**

1. **Reduced Marginal Cost**:
   - Development already completed (HQ paid for it)
   - Proven system (reduced risk)
   - Shared infrastructure (central servers)
   - Minimal customization needed

2. **Incentivize Expansion**:
   - Encourage multi-location rollout
   - Build network effects
   - Increase total contract value (TCV)

3. **Branch Economics**:
   - Smaller branches: 80-120 orders/day (realistic)
   - Revenue: R420,000 - R630,000/month
   - Module cost: R1,999/month
   - **ROI: 210x - 315x return**
   - Still highly profitable

4. **Competitive Advantage**:
   - Lower barrier to entry for additional locations
   - Makes CBD Wellness 24 more competitive vs. competitors

### Enterprise Pricing: R1,499/month (62% Discount)

**Why Enterprise Discount for 3+ Locations?**

1. **Volume Incentive**:
   - Reward commitment to platform
   - Lock in long-term customer
   - Predictable recurring revenue

2. **Total Contract Value (TCV)**:
   - **Example**: 5 locations (1 HQ + 4 branches)
   - HQ: R3,999/month
   - 4 Branches: 4 × R1,499 = R5,996/month
   - **Total: R9,995/month** (vs. R19,995 at full HQ price)

3. **Operational Efficiency**:
   - Centralized support
   - Batch training sessions
   - Shared resources (Redis, MongoDB clusters)
   - Lower support costs per location

4. **Strategic Partnership**:
   - Multi-location clients become partners
   - Feedback loop for product improvement
   - Case studies and testimonials
   - Reference customers for sales

## Pricing Psychology & Strategy

### Anchoring Effect
- HQ price (R3,999) anchors perception of value
- Branch discount (R1,999) feels like 50% savings
- Enterprise discount (R1,499) creates urgency for expansion

### Value-Based Pricing
- Pricing based on customer ROI (200x - 300x return)
- NOT cost-plus pricing (development cost irrelevant to customer)
- Customer focus: "What will I make?" not "What does it cost?"

### Tiered Incentives
- Clear upgrade path: Start with HQ → Add branches → Enterprise tier
- Each tier unlocks better economics
- Encourages growth and expansion

## Pricing Flexibility & Negotiation Guidelines

### When to Offer Custom Pricing
1. **Early Adopters**: First 5 HQ customers get 20% discount (R3,199/month for 6 months)
2. **Annual Prepayment**: 2 months free (R39,990 for 12 months HQ vs. R47,988)
3. **Large Franchises** (5+ locations): Custom negotiation (min. R1,299/month per branch)

### Non-Negotiable Terms
- HQ location MUST pay premium price (no discounts below R3,199/month)
- Branch pricing requires active HQ subscription
- Enterprise tier requires minimum 3 locations

## Summary

**Tiered Pricing Benefits**:
1. **HQ Premium**: Recovers development costs + rewards high-revenue location
2. **Branch Discount**: Incentivizes expansion while maintaining profitability
3. **Enterprise Tier**: Locks in large clients with volume pricing
4. **Flexible**: Accommodates single-location and multi-location businesses
5. **Scalable**: As more branches adopt, total revenue grows significantly

**Projected Annual Revenue** (Conservative):
- Year 1: 10 HQ + 20 branches = (10 × R47,988) + (20 × R23,988) = **R959,640/year**
- Year 2: 25 HQ + 60 branches = (25 × R47,988) + (60 × R23,988) = **R2,639,280/year**

This tiered pricing model balances **value capture** (HQ premium) with **growth incentives** (branch + enterprise discounts).
