## ⚖️ Legal Framework

Under the **Cannabis for Private Purposes Act, 2024** (signed May 2024):
- ✅ Adults may privately use, possess, and cultivate cannabis
- ✅ Private cannabis clubs operate in a legal "grey area"
- ❌ Commercial sale remains technically illegal
- ❌ Public use is prohibited
- 📋 Medical cannabis requires SAHPRA-approved prescription via Section 21

## ⚠️ Important Notes

1. **Legal Grey Area**: Private clubs operate under Constitutional privacy rights but commercial sale remains prohibited
2. **Membership Required**: Most THC-focused establishments require membership (R100-R250/month typical)
3. **ID Required**: All establishments require valid ID (passport for tourists)
4. **Cash Preferred**: Many establishments are cash-only
5. **No Public Use**: Consumption must be in private spaces
6. **Age Restriction**: 18+ (some clubs require 21+)
7. **Medical Cannabis**: Requires Section 21 application through SAHPRA-registered practitioner
