Here is the plain truth of it, and the smart way to fix it — without you and Ilse ever having to hand your books to anyone.

1What you already have — the good news

The model itself is professionally built (by Brightstar) — and it's much more than a revenue forecast. It's a complete model of the whole farm, 10 years out: production and yield by grade, finished-goods stock and what it costs you per kg, debtors and creditors, your buildings and equipment, the loans and the interest, the full profit & loss, balance sheet, cash flow, VAT and tax — and whether you're meeting the bank's covenants. The engine is sound and the history in it is real. We're not throwing any of that away — we're going to feed it the right fuel.

Think of it as your whole farm written in numbers — what grows, what's in stock, what it costs, what you own, what you owe, what you make, and what the taxman and the bank will say. That's exactly why the real numbers matter so much: this one model drives every financial answer about the business.

3Why this matters

A financial model is the tool a bank, an investor, or your board uses to make decisions. Built on guesses, it can quietly point you the wrong way and cost you credibility the moment someone checks it against reality. Built on your real numbers, it becomes a weapon — believable, defensible, and it shows the true path to growth instead of a fairy tale.

4The smart fix — you stay in control of your numbers

You and Ilse never hand your books to us or anyone else. Instead, we give you a simple, private, step-by-step form where you enter your own real figures. And here is the clever part:

  1. It pre-fills what we already know. Your new farm system (going live this week) and the Origin till already capture production, harvest and sales — so those lines are filled in for you automatically.
  2. You only add the confidential bits. The private financial lines — your real costs, loans and accounts — you type in yourself, on your own screen. They stay yours.
  3. It rebuilds the model instantly on those real numbers, ready for the bank or the board.
