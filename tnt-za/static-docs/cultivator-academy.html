2 · What you own · Seo u se okametseng

You take care of the plants in the greenhouse — from when the Nursery Manager transplants them, through veg and flower, to harvest. The system puts your daily jobs on your board. You water, feed, defoliate, scout for pests, and keep the area clean — and you record every job honestly.

Sesotho

U hlokomela limela ka greenhouse — ho tloha ha Nursery Manager a li fallisa (transplant), ho feta nako ea veg le flower, ho fihla kotulong. Sistimi e beha mesebetsi ea hau ea letsatsi le letsatsi bodeng ba hau (board). U nosetsa metsi, u fepa (feed), u tlosa makhasi (defoliate), u batla likokoanyana (pests), 'me u boloka sebaka se hloekile — 'me u ngola mosebetsi o mong le o mong ka botšepehi.

3 · Your daily routine · Mosebetsi oa letsatsi le letsatsi

Every morning, open Checklists

  • Daily Check Sheet — Greenhouse
  • Temp & Humidity — Greenhouse (the readings at the set times)
  • Feeding / Watering record & Defoliating record — never skip one
  • IPM Scouting (look for pests / mould) · Cleaning Checklist · Activity Log

Tap a card → the checklist opens. For each line tap ✓ Done ⚠ Issue N/A. An Issue needs a short note. Fill Date + Initials → Mark Complete.

Sesotho

Hoseng ho hong le ho hong, bula Checklists. U lokela ho etsa:

  • Daily Check Sheet — Greenhouse (tlhahlobo ea letsatsi)
  • Temp & Humidity — Mocheso le Mongobo (u bale ka linako tse behiloeng)
  • Feeding / Watering (ho fepa / ho nosetsa) & Defoliating (ho tlosa makhasi) — u se ke ua siea le e le 'ngoe
  • IPM Scouting (batla likokoanyana / mould) · Cleaning · Activity Log

Tobetsa karete → checklist e buleha. Moleng o mong le o mong tobetsa ✓ Done (E entsoe) ⚠ Issue (Bothata) N/A (Ha ho na). Bothata bo hloka tlhaloso e khutšoanyane. Tlatsa Letsatsi (Date) + Initials → Mark Complete (Qetela).

4 · Receiving transplants · Ho amohela transplant

When the Nursery Manager finishes rooting, the clones come to your greenhouse. Place them in the right bay/row, then take over daily care. Use BayGrid to see your bays, rows and plants — tap a pot to check or flag it.

Sesotho

Ha Nursery Manager a qeta ho melisa metso, li-clone li tla ho greenhouse ea hau. Li behe bay/row e nepahetseng, ebe u nka mosebetsi oa ho li hlokomela letsatsi le letsatsi. Sebelisa BayGrid ho bona li-bay, li-row le limela tsa hau — tobetsa poto ho hlahloba kapena ho e tšoaea (flag).

5 · If a plant is sick · Haeba semela se kula

In BayGrid, tap the plant → Flag (sick) or Cull (dead). High mortality or any flagged issue becomes a deviation that goes to Lou (Head of Cultivation), Loraine (Cultivation Supervisor) and management. If you need supplies, raise a Ticket.

Sesotho

Ka BayGrid, tobetsa semela → Flag (sea kula) kapena Cull (se shoele). Ho shoa ho hoholo kapena bothata bo tšoailoeng e ba deviation e eang ho Lou, Loraine le batsamaisi. Haeba u hloka thepa (supplies), etsa Ticket.
