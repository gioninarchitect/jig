3 · What you own — the Compliance area

In the left sidebar, your home is the Compliance group. Three places, all yours:

  • SOP Library — every SOP and every form. You read, amend, version and sign them off.
  • QMS / Deviations — every deviation and CAPA. Nothing closes without your QA approval first.
  • Inspection Readiness — the live SAHPRA / EU-GMP pack, always current, ready to hand an inspector.
Why this matters to you

On paper, a document only proves itself if you can show who wrote it, who checked it, and that it wasn't changed behind your back. The system does that automatically — so the proof is built in, not chased after.

4 · The SOP Library — your master documents

You have full control

  • Amend wording — open any SOP → Amend SOP → edit → Save amendment. It saves as a new version with your sign-off.
  • Edit a form's checklist — add, remove or reword any line item; mark items required or optional.
  • Update citations — the regulatory sources (EU-GMP, SAHPRA, Pharmacy Council, SA law) are listed; you decide what's grounded and active. The system never invents a citation — a human (you) confirms every one.
Version history — you can never lose a document

Every edit is snapshotted before it saves. Open any SOP → Version history → you see every previous version with date and author → Restore rolls it back in one tap (and that restore is itself reversible). A wrong edit is never permanent.

5 · Deviations & CAPA — your sign-off is the gate

When something goes wrong on the floor — a failed check, a weight variance, high mortality — it becomes a deviation and lands in your QMS queue.

  • You review and approve the investigation and corrective action.
  • A deviation cannot be closed until you've approved it — the Responsible Pharmacist signs off only after QA. The rail is enforced in the system, not by memory.
  • A flagged issue automatically assigns remedial training to the person involved — and blocks their next sign-off until it's done.
