2 · What you own · Zomwe mukuyang'anira

You run the Mother Room and the Clone Room — the start of every plant's life. The system puts your daily jobs on your board automatically; you just do them and sign off. You also allocate the cleaning to the general staff and sign for it.

Chichewa

Mukuyang'anira Mother Room (chipinda cha mbewu zazimayi) ndi Clone Room (chipinda cha ana a mbewu) — chiyambi cha moyo wa mbewu iliyonse. Sisitimu imayika ntchito zanu za tsiku ndi tsiku pa board yanu yokha; inu mungozichita ndi kusayina (sign off). Mumaperekanso ntchito yoyeretsa kwa antchito ena, kenako mumasayina.

3 · Your daily routine · Ntchito za tsiku ndi tsiku

Every morning, open Checklists (Tasks)

  • Daily Check Sheet — Clone Room & Mother Bay
  • Temp & Humidity — Clone Room (3 readings: 08:00 / 11:50 / 16:30)
  • Cleaning Checklist — Clone Room / Mother Bay (allocate a cleaner, then sign)
  • Jiffy Stock Sheet · Motherbay Foliar Application Log

Tap a card → it opens the full checklist. For each line tap ✓ Done ⚠ Issue N/A. An Issue asks for a quick note. Fill the Date + your Initials → Mark Complete.

Chichewa

M'mawa uliwonse, tsegulani Checklists (Tasks). Muyenera kuchita:

  • Daily Check Sheet — kuyang'ana kwa tsiku ndi tsiku (Clone Room & Mother Bay)
  • Temp & Humidity — Kutentha ndi Chinyezi mu Clone Room (kuwerenga katatu: 08:00 / 11:50 / 16:30)
  • Cleaning Checklist — perekani kwa woyeretsa, kenako sayinani
  • Jiffy Stock Sheet · Motherbay Foliar Application Log

Dinani kadi → imatsegula checklist yonse. Pa mzere uliwonse dinani ✓ Done (Yatha) ⚠ Issue (Vuto) N/A (Palibe). Vuto limafuna chidziwitso chachifupi. Lembani Tsiku (Date) + zilembo zanu (Initials) → Mark Complete (Maliza).

4 · Starting a clone batch · Kuyamba batch ya ma clone

1

Cloning → New Cloning Job

Pick the mother you cut from (e.g. SL-01 in MR1), enter how many cuttings. The batch number is automatic (CT-2026-001 is the genesis batch).

Chichewa

Sankhani mayi (mother) amene mwadula (mwachitsanzo SL-01 mu MR1), lembani chiwerengero cha ma cutting. Nambala ya batch imadzipanga yokha (CT-2026-001 ndi batch yoyamba/genesis).

2

The system does the rest

It loads your 14-day rooting schedule onto your board — daily Temp & Humidity (with the right targets per day) + Mortality checks W1/W2/W3 + the Transplant date. Lou gets a "build the grow calendar" ticket; the cultivators get a heads-up to prep the greenhouse.

Chichewa

Imayika ndandanda wa masiku 14 a kumera mizu pa board yanu — Kutentha ndi Chinyezi tsiku ndi tsiku (ndi zolinga zoyenera pa tsiku) + kuyang'ana kufa kwa mbewu (Mortality) W1/W2/W3 + tsiku la Transplant (kusamutsa). Lou amalandira ticket yopanga "grow calendar"; alimi (cultivators) amadziwitsidwa kuti akonze greenhouse.

3

Track rooting & deaths honestly

Each day capture the readings. On the mortality checks, record any deaths — that feeds the owner dashboard and triggers a deviation if it's high. Real numbers only.

Chichewa

Tsiku lililonse lembani ziwerengero. Pa kuyang'ana kufa, lembani mbewu zonse zomwe zafa — izi zimapita ku dashboard ya mwiniwake ndipo zimayambitsa deviation ngati zafa zambiri. Lembani manambala oona okha.

5 · Transplant handover · Kupereka pa Transplant (~tsiku 15)

When rooting is done, the Transplant task is due. Plants move to the greenhouse and the cultivators take over daily care. You've handed the baton — your job for that batch is done; the next batch begins.

Chichewa

Mizu ikamaliza kumera, ntchito ya Transplant imafika. Mbewu zimapita ku greenhouse ndipo alimi (cultivators) amatenga udindo wosamalira tsiku ndi tsiku. Mwapereka udindo — ntchito yanu pa batch imeneyo yatha; batch yotsatira imayamba.

6 · If something's wrong · Ngati chinthu chalakwika

Raise a Ticket (Tickets → New). Anything you flag as an Issue on a check, or a high mortality, becomes a deviation that routes to Lou (Head of Cultivation), Loraine (Cultivation Supervisor) and management — so you're never carrying a problem alone.

Chichewa

Tsegulani Ticket (Tickets → New). Chilichonse chomwe mwachilemba ngati Issue (Vuto) pa kuyang'ana, kapena kufa kwambiri kwa mbewu, chimakhala deviation chomwe chimapita kwa Lou (Head of Cultivation), Loraine (Cultivation Supervisor) ndi oyang'anira — kotero simunyamula vuto nokha.
