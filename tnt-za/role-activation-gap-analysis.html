Two modules every role gets

The activation layer you asked for — the same for every dashboard.

1 · Interactive in-dashboard training

Training lives inside each role's dashboard, on the real screens — not a separate course or a PDF. A guided, step-by-step walkthrough of that role's actual daily workflow, that anyone can finish without help.

  • Role-aware: Nursery Staff are walked through plant register → daily check → phase change; an RP through prescription intake → dispense → sign-off.
  • Interactive coach marks / guided steps on the live UI, with "try it" checkpoints and progress tracking per person.
  • Tied to the competency record — completing the walkthrough logs training (links to the SOP-training gate).
  • Re-runnable any time from the dashboard ("Show me how").

2 · In-dashboard bug & idea assistant → your backlog + kanban

Every role gets a small AI assistant in their dashboard to report a bug or suggest an enhancement in plain language. It captures context (role, page, what they were doing) and files it straight to a working backlog and a draggable kanban board — for you.

  • One tap from any screen: "Report a bug / suggest something" → the assistant asks 1–2 clarifying questions, auto-tags role + page + severity.
  • Flows to an owner-only board: New → Triaged → In progress → Done, drag-and-drop, filter by role / facility / type (bug vs enhancement).
  • Turns every person on the farm + every network pharmacy into a live feedback channel — you see exactly what's breaking and what they want, in one place.

The three critical blockers

Fix these first — they unblock multiple roles.
1 · Daily-check backend — unblocks all of Cultivation's day-to-day. Wire the zone config + submission + audit trail.
2 · QA-decision endpoint — unblocks both Processing and QA/Compliance sign-off and batch release.
3 · Section 21 dispensing workflow — the Responsible Pharmacist can't operate without it (register + intake + signature).
