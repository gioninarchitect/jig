What Is TnT-ZA?

TnT-ZA is a digital track and trace system that follows every plant, every gram, and every person involved in cannabis production — from the moment a cutting is taken from a mother plant until the final product reaches the customer.

In simple terms: Every action in the facility is recorded digitally. Every weight is logged. Every handover is documented. The system knows where everything is, who touched it, and when. Nothing goes unaccounted for.

The Problem We Solve

Without TnT-ZA

  • Paper records — easy to lose
  • No real-time visibility
  • Weight discrepancies found too late
  • SAHPRA audits take days to prepare
  • No product traceability
  • Accountability relies on trust

With TnT-ZA

  • Every gram tracked digitally
  • Real-time dashboards per role
  • Weight variances detected instantly
  • Site Master File ready in seconds
  • Full QR provenance for customers
  • Every action tied to a person + time
