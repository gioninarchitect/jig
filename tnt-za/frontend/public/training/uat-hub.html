How to log in

Login flow (2 steps)

▼

Step 1: Enter the email address → tap "Request PIN"

Step 2: The PIN is logged on the server. For demo, use the seed PINs above. Enter the 6-digit PIN → you're in.

Note: The seed PINs in the table are pre-hashed into the database. When you request a PIN via the login page, a NEW PIN is generated. For demo testing, check the server logs: pm2 logs tnt-za --lines 10

UAT Checklist — Test Everything

Dashboard & World Model

▼
4 stat cards show real numbers (50 plants, batches, anomalies, quota %)
Phase bar chart shows plant distribution (SEEDLING through PACKAGED)
Activity feed shows last 10 audit entries with timestamps
Weight variance panel (red) shows open CONTAINER_WEIGHT_VARIANCE anomaly
3 risk gauges show: Diversion Risk, Compliance Score, Weight Integrity
Inference alerts show if any active warnings
Facilities table shows ILCO Farm with plant/batch counts
Setup banner shows "Set up your facility" CTA (for FM+ roles)

Plants

▼
Plant list shows 50 plants with ID, strain, phase, zone
Search works (try "Durban" or "ZA-000")
Phase filter works (select HARVESTED, DRYING etc)
Click a plant → detail page with phase timeline + weights
Genealogy shows mother plant + clones for first 10 plants
Mobile — table becomes cards on phone

Containers

▼
Container list shows 10 containers (BIN, RACK, BAG, JAR)
Click a container → event timeline (LOAD, MOVE, HANDOVER, UNLOAD)
Weight chart shows weight over time for containers with >1 event
BIN-001 has the suspicious unload (30% weight loss)

Batches & Lab

▼
4 batch cards with different statuses (ACTIVE, IN_TESTING, QUARANTINED, RELEASED)
Click a batch → 8-test grid shows pass/fail/pending
B-2026-004 (RELEASED) has COA issued + all 8 tests passing
B-2026-003 (QUARANTINED) has failed pesticide test
Chain of custody shows container events per batch
Lab page shows testing status per batch with progress bars

Compliance, Security, QMS, Audit

▼
Compliance — quota gauge, open anomalies list, destruction log (2 events)
Security — transport manifest, open anomalies, destruction records
QMS — 2 SOPs with versions, 1 open deviation, 2 equipment with calibration dates
Audit — 25+ entries with user, action, entity, timestamp. CSV export link.

Onboarding Wizard

▼
Accessible from sidebar "Setup Wizard" and dashboard banner
Step 0 — 4 scenario cards (Indoor, Outdoor, Manufacturer, Research)
Step 1 — Facility name, address, GPS "Use My Location", grow type toggle
Step 2 — License number, expiry, quota
Step 3 — Zone template or custom zones
Step 4 — Quick-add strain chips + custom strains
Steps 5-8 — Equipment, Team, SOPs, Partners (all skippable)
Mobile — works on phone with big touch targets
