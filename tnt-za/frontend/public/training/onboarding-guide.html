Onboarding

Facility Setup Guide

How to configure your cultivation facility in TnT-ZA — from first login to tracking your first plant. One wizard, 8 steps, under 15 minutes.

~15 minutes to complete
One thing per screen. The wizard asks one question at a time. Big touch targets for phone use. Skip optional steps and come back later. Smart defaults pre-fill most fields.
Step 0 — Choose Your Scenario

The first screen asks what type of operation you run. This configures everything that follows — zone templates, license types, container types.

The 8 Steps
1

Where is your grow?

Your facility's identity and physical location.
  • Facility name (e.g. "ILCO Farm Potchefstroom")
  • Physical address (street, city, province)
  • GPS coordinates (tap "Use My Location" — auto-detects)
  • Grow type — Indoor / Greenhouse / Outdoor (one-tap toggle)
2

Your permits & licenses

SAHPRA license, Section 22A permit, INCB quota.
  • Section 22C(1)(b) license number
  • License expiry date
  • Section 22A permit number (optional)
  • GMP certificate number (optional)
  • INCB annual plant quota (plant registration blocks at 100%)
3

Set up your rooms

Define zones where plants and containers move through.
  • Quick option: "Use Standard Layout" — pre-creates 6 zones based on your scenario
  • Custom: Add your own zones with name, type, and capacity
  • Zone types: PROPAGATION GROW DRY TRIM CURE PROCESS PACK STORAGE QUARANTINE
4

What do you grow?

Register your cannabis strains. Quick-add from popular SA strains.
  • Strain name (quick-add chips: Durban Poison, Swazi Gold, Malawi Gold...)
  • Species — Sativa / Indica / Hybrid
  • Chemo-type — THC-dominant / CBD-dominant / Balanced
  • Source / seed bank (optional — for SAHPRA traceability)
  • Expected yield per plant (optional — enables yield deviation detection)
5

Register your scales

Weight tracking is the core of TnT-ZA. Scales must be registered and calibrated.
  • Equipment name (e.g. "Mettler Toledo XS205 — Drying Room")
  • Last calibration date
  • Calibration interval (30, 60, 90 days)
  • System alerts when calibration is due within 7 days
6

Invite your team

Add cultivators, lab techs, security officers. They get login PINs by email.
  • Name and email (email becomes their login)
  • Role — CULTIVATOR LAB_TECH SECURITY_OFFICER VIEWER
  • Each role sees only what they need (RBAC-gated sidebar)
  • PIN auto-generated and emailed on invite
7

Standard Operating Procedures

SOPs are required by SAHPRA GMP. Use our templates or create your own.
  • Recommended: "Use ILCO Standard SOPs" — 5 templates pre-loaded
  • Harvesting & Weighing | Container Handling | Drying Room | Lab Sampling | Destruction
  • Or skip and create your own later in QMS
8

Your compliance partners

Optional — SAPS station, lab partner, waste company.
  • Local SAPS station (for destruction witnessing)
  • SAPS contact officer
  • Lab testing partner
  • Waste management company
After Setup
You're ready to grow. The completion screen shows your facility summary and 3 action buttons: "Register Your First Plant" | "Register Your First Container" | "Go to Dashboard"
