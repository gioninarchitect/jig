Tenant Admin

TnT-ZA Training Guide

Business-level oversight of the entire tenant. You monitor compliance, review anomalies, and ensure the facility operates within regulatory boundaries.

You receive all weight variance alerts. Any container weight loss beyond threshold is reported to you immediately alongside the Super Admin and Facility Manager.
Getting Started

How to Log In

▼

TnT-ZA uses PIN-to-email authentication. No passwords.

  • Go to https://tntilco.cleva-ai.co.za
  • Enter your email address
  • Check your email for a 6-digit PIN (expires in 5 minutes)
  • Enter the PIN to log in

Your session lasts 24 hours.

What You See (Level 4 Access)

▼

Your sidebar shows 10 sections:

  • Dashboard — KPIs, risk gauges, weight alerts, activity feed
  • Scan — Photo scan to identify plants/containers
  • Plants — Full plant registry with search and filters
  • Containers — All containers with event timelines
  • Batches — Batch cards, lab status, COA, chain of custody
  • Facilities — Facility overview with quota and zones
  • Audit — Immutable audit log with CSV export
  • Lab — Testing progress per batch
  • Compliance — Quota, anomalies, destruction log, permits
  • Security — Transport manifests, open anomalies

You do not see the Users management page (Super Admin only).

Key Responsibilities

Monitoring Weight Variance Alerts

▼

The red panel on the dashboard shows open weight variance anomalies.

  • Every container is weighed at every zone transition
  • If loss exceeds threshold (5% bins/bags/jars, 15% racks) — anomaly fires
  • You are notified alongside Super Admin and Facility Manager
  • Alerts cannot be dismissed — must be resolved with investigation notes

Go to Compliance → Open Anomalies to review and follow up with the Facility Manager.

Reviewing Compliance Status

▼
  • Check Compliance page for quota usage, permit expiry, pending destructions
  • Review the 3 risk gauges: Diversion Risk, Compliance Score, Weight Integrity
  • If Compliance Score drops below 70, investigate open anomalies and pending items
  • Ensure destruction events are confirmed by the Facility Manager

Overseeing Batch & Lab Status

▼
  • Check Batches page for batch status (ACTIVE, IN_TESTING, QUARANTINED, RELEASED)
  • Quarantined batches need attention — failed lab tests require retesting
  • Verify COAs are issued for released batches before distribution
  • Review chain-of-custody for any batch before signing off

Transport Oversight

▼
  • Check Security page for transport manifests
  • If a transport arrives >130% of expected duration, a TRANSPORT_TIME anomaly is auto-created
  • Follow up with Security Officer and transporter
Daily & Weekly Routine

Daily Checklist

▼
  • Open Dashboard — check for red weight variance panel
  • Review risk gauges (all 3 should be green)
  • Check active inference alerts
  • Review notification bell for overnight activity
  • Check quota percentage

Weekly Checklist

▼
  • Review all resolved anomalies — are investigation notes adequate?
  • Check permit expiry dates on Compliance page
  • Review SOP acknowledgement counts on QMS page
  • Check equipment calibration schedule
  • Export audit CSV for filing
