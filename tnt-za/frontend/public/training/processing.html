Processing / Trimmer

TnT-ZA Training Guide

You turn wet harvest into clean, weighed, packed product. Every gram you handle is reconciled — issued weight in must equal trimmed, waste and returned weight out. Your weights register is the chain-of-custody.

Issued must reconcile to returned. At the end of every trim shift your row must balance: Issued = Flos + M-L + Trimmings + Sticks/Seeds + Untrimmed. A per-trimmer gap over 5% is a flag and will be queried by QA.
Getting Started

What You See (Processing Access)

▼

Your menu is built for the processing floor:

  • My Shift — clock in/out, your assigned trim sessions
  • Tickets & Checklists — jobs and cleaning/task lists assigned to you
  • Scan & Tag — scan container and batch labels at every step
  • Batches & Containers — the product you are working on

You do not see Lab results, COA issue, or QMS authoring — those belong to QA and management. That is by design.

The Processing Flow (Your Core Job)

1. Receiving Wet Material

▼

When harvested plants arrive in the receiving area:

  • Scan the container — confirm the batch number and strain on screen match the label
  • Confirm the load matches what was sent (type of stock, weight)
  • If anything does not match, it is rejected and segregated — do not move it into drying
  • Receiving must be signed — never receive product without recording who received it

2. Drying (Batch Record)

▼

Drying runs against the batch's drying record:

  • Record strain, batch number, batch size in
  • Keep drying conditions inside the target envelope — temperature 15–25°C, relative humidity 50–60% (confirm the current ILCO target on the room SOP)
  • Conditions out of range for more than ~30 minutes must be raised as a deviation
  • Record the actual dry output weight when the batch leaves drying — this is the input to trimming

3. Trimming & Your Weights Register

▼

This is where your accountability lives. On the Trim Sessions page:

  • You are issued a weight to trim — recorded against your name
  • At the end you return, by category: Flos (flower), M-L, Trimmings, Sticks/Seeds, Untrimmed
  • The system totals your row and checks it against your issued weight
  • Every trimmer who worked the shift must have a row — no missing rows
  • The grand total must reconcile back to the batch input
Think of it as a balance: nothing should "disappear." If your numbers don't add up, weigh again before you sign off — a clean register protects you.

4. Weighing & Pack-out

▼
  • Final product is weighed in the weighing & pack area and reconciled: Gross → Waste → Net → Loss%
  • Scan the container at pack-out so the weight is tied to the batch
  • Large unexplained losses fire a weight-variance alert to management — expect to be asked what happened
Quality Discipline (Why It Matters)

Daily Balance Verification

▼
  • Before weighing, the balance must be levelled and stabilised and checked with a known reference mass
  • If the balance fails the check: take it offline, raise a deviation, log the service call — do not keep weighing on a suspect scale
  • A wrong scale makes every batch weight wrong, so this 2-minute check protects the whole day

Cleaning & Sanitizer Rotation

▼
  • Cleaning is logged per area with a date, per-task tick, and a QC/QA sign-off
  • Rotate sanitizers — do not use the same chemical two days running (resistance risk)
  • Wash bay and hallways are shared cross-flow points — a missed clean there contaminates clean rooms downstream
  • Record it as you do it, not at the end of the week from memory
Daily Routine

Your Day, Start to Finish

▼
  • Clock in on My Shift
  • Check Checklists — your cleaning and task list for today
  • Verify the balance before any weighing
  • Scan every container in and out — never move product without a scan
  • Record your trim weights as you finish each issue, while the numbers are fresh
  • Reconcile and sign your weights register before you leave
  • Clock out
