Super Admin

TnT-ZA Training Guide

Full system access. You see everything, control everything, and are responsible for the integrity of the entire platform.

Weight variance alerts come directly to you. Any container weight loss beyond threshold triggers an immediate notification. You cannot dismiss these from the dashboard — they must be investigated and resolved with notes.
Getting Started

How to Log In

▼

TnT-ZA uses PIN-to-email authentication. No passwords.

  • Go to https://tnt.cleva-ai.co.za
  • Enter your email address
  • Check your email for a 6-digit PIN (expires in 5 minutes)
  • Enter the PIN to log in

Your session lasts 24 hours. After that, you'll need a new PIN.

What You See (Full Access)

▼

As Super Admin, your sidebar shows all 11 sections:

  • Dashboard — KPIs, phase chart, activity feed, weight alerts, risk gauges
  • Plants — All 50+ plants, search, filter, phase transitions
  • Containers — All bins/racks/bags/jars, event timelines, weight charts
  • Batches — Batch cards, lab status, COA download, chain of custody
  • Facilities — Facility cards with quota, zones, counts

  • Security — Open anomalies, transport manifests, destruction records

Critical Responsibilities

Weight Variance Alerts

▼

The red panel on the dashboard shows all open weight variance anomalies. This is the most critical feature in TnT-ZA.

  • Every container is weighed at every zone transition
  • If weight loss exceeds the threshold (5% for bins/bags/jars, 15% for drying racks), an anomaly is created automatically
  • You, the Tenant Admin, and the Facility Manager are all notified simultaneously
  • These alerts cannot be dismissed — they must be resolved with investigation notes

To resolve: go to Compliance → Open Anomalies and click Resolve. You must enter what you found.

Audit Log & Hash Chain Verification

▼

Every action in TnT-ZA is logged in an append-only audit trail. No entry can ever be updated or deleted.

  • Each entry is linked to the previous via a SHA-256 hash chain
  • If anyone tampers with a past entry, the chain breaks and verification fails
  • Only you (Super Admin) can run GET /api/audit/verify to check integrity
  • Export the full log as CSV for regulatory submissions

Quota Management

▼

Only Super Admin can update the INCB annual quota for a facility.

  • The quota is shown on the dashboard as a percentage
  • At 70%, 85%, and 95% — automatic alerts are created
  • At 100% — new plant registration is blocked
  • Update via PATCH /api/compliance/quota/:facilityId

Risk Scores

▼

The dashboard shows 3 computed risk gauges:

  • Diversion Risk (0-100, higher = worse) — based on weight variances + open anomalies
  • Compliance Score (0-100, higher = better) — based on anomaly count + pending destructions
  • Weight Integrity (0-100, higher = better) — based on container variance data

These update automatically from the World Model. If diversion risk goes above 50, the system flags a Potential Diversion Pattern alert.

Daily Workflow

Morning Checklist

▼
  • Open Dashboard — check for red weight variance panel
  • Review risk gauges — all 3 should be green
  • Check active alerts (inference engine) for any warnings
  • Review notifications bell for overnight activity
  • Check quota percentage — plan if approaching limit

Weekly Checklist

▼
  • Run audit chain verification
  • Export audit CSV for records
  • Review all resolved anomalies — check investigation notes are adequate
  • Check permit expiry dates (Compliance page)
  • Review equipment calibration schedule (QMS page)
