The Quality Gate (Your Core Job)

Lab Results & Certificates of Analysis

▼
  • On Lab & COA, record each test against the batch (test type, result, pass/fail)
  • A batch that fails testing must not move forward — it goes to quarantine
  • When testing passes and records are complete, you issue the COA — the document that says this batch is fit for release
  • The COA carries a QR code and is part of the permanent record

Quarantine & Release

▼

You hold the gate between "made" and "released":

  • Quarantine a batch the moment there is doubt — failed test, open deviation, unresolved weight variance
  • A quarantined batch is locked from dispatch until you clear it
  • Release only when: testing passed, records complete, deviations closed
When in doubt, quarantine. It is always cheaper to hold a batch than to recall it.

The Sign-Off Chain

▼

Batch records move through a chain — your review is the quality step in the middle:

  • Production complete — the processing team finishes and signs the batch record
  • QA review — you check the record is complete and correct, and that conditions stayed in range
  • Release — final authorisation that the batch may leave

An incomplete sign-off chain is one of the most common audit findings. A blank signature box is a finding — chase it before it becomes one.

QMS — Keeping the System Honest

Deviations & CAPA

▼
  • When work departs from an SOP, a deviation is recorded with a severity
  • You add the root cause and the CAPA (corrective & preventive action), then close it
  • A deviation linked to a batch keeps that batch in quarantine until closed
  • Repeated deviations on the same SOP signal a real process problem — not just paperwork

SOP Library & Training Acknowledgement

▼
  • Every SOP is versioned in the SOP Library; staff acknowledge each version
  • You can see who has and has not acknowledged — chase the gaps
  • When an SOP changes, the old acknowledgements no longer count — staff must re-acknowledge
  • This is your evidence that the team was trained on the current method

Equipment Calibration

▼
  • Scales and instruments are registered with calibration dates
  • The system warns when calibration is due within 7 days
  • A failed balance check should appear here as a deviation — confirm it was taken offline
Daily Routine

Your Quality Walk-through

▼
  • Anomalies — any open weight-variance or compliance alerts overnight?
  • Lab — pending tests to action; any failed batches to quarantine?
  • QMS — deviations awaiting root cause / CAPA closure
  • SOP Library — acknowledgement gaps to chase
  • Calibration — anything due this week
  • Review and clear (or hold) any batch waiting on your sign-off
