🏛 SAHPRA Compliance

Batch Created — Auto-log in production register
Quarantine — 7 auto-tasks: isolate, notify SAHPRA 24hrs, sample, document, root cause, cleaning, equipment
Destruction — SAPS witness. Weigh, photograph, sign, file, update INCB
COA — Auto-attached to batch. PDF + QR. Required before sale
Dispatch — Manifest with Section 22C permit ref
Monthly INCB — Reconcile produced vs destroyed vs stock vs dispatched
Spray — Per-batch: chemical, dilution, PPE, applicator, time, re-entry
Calibration — Weekly: scales, pH, EC, temp/humidity sensors
Daily Inspection — Entry/exit, cameras, PPE, pest traps, fire
Room Cleaning — Per room per day. Grow, dry, trim, pack
