What Did We Build?

We built a digital system that tracks everything on the farm — every plant, every gram of product, every person who touches it, and every task that needs doing. It works on your phone.

Think of it like this: Before, you had paper checklists, WhatsApp messages, and spreadsheets. Now everything is in one app. Staff opens their phone, sees what they need to do today, does it, and the system records everything automatically. Management sees everything in real time.

Why Does This Matter?

For SAHPRA Compliance

  • Every gram tracked from mother plant to customer
  • Site Master File ready to export as PDF — always up to date
  • When something goes wrong (quarantine), the system auto-creates 7 tasks
  • Destruction requires SAPS witness — system enforces this
  • Audit trails are tamper-proof — nobody can alter records

For the Business

  • Know exactly how much product at any stage
  • See which strains perform best
  • Spot problems early — weight discrepancies flagged immediately
  • Staff accountability — every action tied to a person
  • No more lost information — everything searchable
  • Owner dashboard shows all business units

How Product Is Tracked — In Plain English

#StepWhat HappensWhat the System Does
1StrainWe decide what to growRecords strain performance over time
2MotherThe plant we take cuttings fromTracks health, clones, success rates
3Clone20 cuttings from the motherAuto-creates IDs: M01-01 to M01-20
4Rooting2 weeks for roots to growTracks survivors + deaths + why they died
5TransplantSurvivors go to greenhouseBatch created, plant IDs activated, bay filled
6GrowingVeg → Flip → Flower (3 months)Daily tasks from calendar, feeding logged
7HarvestCut and weighWET WEIGHT = baseline for all tracking
8-13ProcessingDry → Debuc → Trim → CureWeight at EVERY handover. RP signs each stage.
14Lab & QA8 tests must all passCOA generated. Batch = RELEASED
15-17Sale → DeliverCustomer gets productScan QR = full history back to mother
Weight Protection: Every time product changes hands, it gets weighed. If the difference is more than 2%, the system immediately alerts Ray, Ilze, and Coenie. This prevents loss and diversion.

The Batch Number Tells the Story

DP-M01-20260301 = Durban Poison, from Mother M01, cloned 1 March 2026. Anyone can read that and know exactly where this product came from.

When Something Dies

The system records how many died, why (pest, mould, root rot, etc.), and creates a destruction ticket. Dead material requires FM authorization + SAPS witness. Over time, this data shows which strains/mothers have problems — helping improve future grows.

The Smart Features

1. Smart Tickets — Problems Find the Right Person

When someone raises a ticket, the system automatically assigns it to the right person. No more "who do I tell about this?"

  • Pest in Bay 2? → Goes to Head of Cultivation
  • Equipment broken? → Goes to Maintenance Manager
  • Need supplies? → Goes to Owner for approval

2. Auto-Escalation — Nothing Gets Forgotten

  • After 4 hours no response → priority bumps to HIGH
  • After 24 hours → bumps to CRITICAL + escalated to FM
  • System adds a note explaining the escalation

3. Smart Notifications — Each Person Sees What Matters

PersonNotifications
Ilze / CoeniePending approvals, weight alerts, stale tickets
RayCritical tickets, weight anomalies, overdue tasks
LouMother health checks, clone mortality, calendar alerts
JannetteActive trim sessions, weight reconciliation issues
MaintenanceCalibration due, faulty assets, low stock
RPProducts waiting for sign-off
Staff"Your shift hasn't started" + overdue tasks

4. Grow Calendar → Auto-Tasks

The calendar knows what needs doing each day. FM taps one button → tickets created for the next 3 days. Staff sees them in their shift view.

5. SOP Library → Training Tracking

Every SOP is linked to the roles that need to know it. The system tracks who's trained and who hasn't. Shown as a percentage — "85% of staff trained on this SOP".

6. Forecasting

Dashboard shows what's coming in the next 3 days. Critical events (FLIP day, Harvest day) highlighted so management can plan ahead.
