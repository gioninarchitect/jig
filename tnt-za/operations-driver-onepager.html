The Operations Driver

Every role, run by an AI co-pilot.
A shift from a system that stores data to one that drives the work. Each role gets an AI co-pilot grounded in its SOPs and KPIs that says what to do now, raises the ticket the moment something slips, and allocates the day's work — across the farm and every network pharmacy.

Most of the engine is already built

Per-role AI Q&A 15+ roles KPI engine targets + live Smart tickets auto-route + escalate SOP → training + tasks World model + risk Event bus

The Driver connects them into a loop. It is thin glue + one rules engine — not a rebuild.

What a role sees

The co-pilot doesn't wait to be asked. It opens with "Here's your shift" — today's SOP checklist, KPI status (green/amber), and the action queue. Ask it anything in your role's language; it answers from your SOPs & live state, and can act (close a ticket, complete a task, accept an allocation).

FM · Nursery Mgr · Nursery Staff · Cultivation Mgr/Head Grower · Processing · QA · RP

Driven, not passive:

· Clone rooting drops below 85% → ticket to the Nursery Manager.
· An SOP goes overdue → training + task auto-raised.
· Weight variance fires → QA + FM alerted, batch flagged.
· The day's SOP workload → auto-allocated across the shift.

How we roll it out

1
Close the loop — events + tick + the rules engine produce a read-only "what to do now" feed. Start here prove the brain before it acts.
2
Wire it to act — drivers become tickets & tasks, auto-routed.
3
Per-role Action Queue + co-pilot — SOPs & KPIs injected, with memory.
4
Auto-allocation — fill the shift from the day's SOP workload.
5
Executable chat — the co-pilot runs actions, with audit.
Wire the heartbeat into a rules engine that reads the KPIs, anomalies and SOPs you already compute, turns breaches into the tickets you already auto-route, and hands every role a live "what to do now" — inside the assistant you already have.
