The cash-up now reconciles the card machine too

Until now the day-end only reconciled cash (count the drawer vs expected). The card figure was shown but never checked against the Speedpoint. Now you can balance the card machine against the till, the same way you balance cash.

How to use it Cashier

  1. Do the cash count as normal (notes & coins).
  2. Below the cash total there's a new box: Card machine (Speedpoint) — optional. It shows the system card sales for the shift.
  3. Pull the Speedpoint end-of-day / batch total off the card machine and type it into the box.
  4. It shows the difference live — ✓ balances if they match, or the amount out if not.
  5. Press Count Complete and finish the cash-up as usual.
It's optional. If you leave the card box blank, the cash-up works exactly as before — nothing changes and it will never stop you closing the till.

What it controls Owner

When the cashier enters the Speedpoint total, the system compares it to the recorded card sales and stores a card variance:

  1. The card figure & variance show on the Variance step and on the Z-Report.
  2. If the card doesn't balance, the close is flagged for owner review and an email goes to ray@ilcofarming.co.za — exactly like a cash variance.
  3. The who / when / figures are written to the audit trail.

This closes a real gap: card settlements that came up short (or a missed batch) would previously never have surfaced.

Example — end of shift:
  System card sales  = R 4,800.00
  Speedpoint batch   = R 4,800.00  → ✓ balances
  Speedpoint batch   = R 4,600.00  → −R 200.00 · flagged for owner review
