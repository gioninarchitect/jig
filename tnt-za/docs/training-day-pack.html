1 · How everyone logs in & the master list

Everyone logs in the same way, on their own phone or tablet:

  1. Go to tntilco.cleva-ai.co.za
  2. Enter your login email (below) → tap send
  3. Open the email → tap the gold “Log in to ILCO” button → enter the 6-digit PIN
  4. You land on your own dashboard.
