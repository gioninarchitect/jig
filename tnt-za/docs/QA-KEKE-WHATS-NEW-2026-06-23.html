Your QA work board & QMS — what's changed

We rebuilt the quality side around how you actually run QA. This is your GMP control system, in one place.

The big change: your work board now shows your quality work — not everyone else's daily checklists. Deviations to review and sign-offs come to you; the rest of the farm's tasks stay on their own boards.

1Your work board = your QA queue

When you open your board, you land on the QA view: every deviation (from any department) plus your QA / Lab work and sign-offs. The hundreds of daily cleaning & cultivation checklists other roles complete no longer clutter your screen — you see what needs your quality decision.

2Deviations — raise, approve, close

Deviations move across your board like cards:

Raised (anyone)→ You approve (QA)→ RP closes (with CAPA)

A deviation can't be closed without a CAPA (corrective/preventive action) — quality is enforced, not optional.

3Your QMS hub — all live

SOP registerVersioned SOPs + staff acknowledgement tracking
Deviations & CAPARaise, review, approve, close with corrective action
Equipment calibrationCalibration schedule + due dates
EU-GMP control registryEvery control mapped to its EU-GMP source
QMS recordsYour quality records, in one auditable place

4You can still see everything

Oversight hasn't gone away — you can still view the whole facility's activity through the Oversight view. The difference is it's now a separate view you choose, not mixed into your daily to-do.
