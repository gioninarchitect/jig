Proving the seed-to-sale pipeline to SAHPRA & SAPC

Goal: demonstrate one cannabis batch flowing cultivation → processing → lab/COA → dispatch → pharmacy → Section-21 dispense, fully traced and audit-clean, to the medicines regulator (SAHPRA) and the pharmacy council (SAPC). Live demo + compliance document pack + on-site walk-through.

🔴 The one thing that must happen first — a proof run. The system has the full capability, but the live DB has 0 batches and 0 COAs — the pipeline has never been run end-to-end with real data. You cannot "prove" a pipeline that has no finished, tested, dispensed batch to show. Week 1 is dedicated to walking one real (pilot) batch the whole way so there is an actual COA and an actual Section-21 dispense on screen.

The three ways we present it

1 · Live system demo. Walk the pilot batch on the real system, screen-shared or on the POSBANK: BayGrid lineage → harvest → batch → lab → COA (open the PDF) → dispatch → pharmacy receipt → Section-21 dispense. Then show the audit trail reproducing that exact batch's history, and the RBAC (each role sees only its own actions).
2 · Compliance document pack. A branded PDF mapping every regulator concern → the live feature that satisfies it, with screenshots + the pilot batch's COA and audit extract as evidence. Two sections: SAHPRA (cultivation/GMP/quality) and SAPC (pharmacy/dispensing). Regulatory copy specialist-reviewed before issue.
3 · On-site walk-through. Regulators at the farm + shop; each physical step shown against the system in context — the mother bay ↔ BayGrid, the processing rooms ↔ container tracking, the pharmacy ↔ Section-21 dispense. (This is where Loraine's Phase-3 room-by-room view pays off — parked but relevant.)

Gaps & decisions before we present

APilot batch source. Which strain/mother do we run through as the proof batch? Is there physical stock far enough along to produce a genuine harvest→COA, or is this a documented dry-run?
BLab & COA. A real COA needs a real lab result. Do we have a lab partner result to load, or do we demonstrate the COA mechanism with a clearly-marked sample?
CSpecialist sign-off. Who is the medicines-law + pharmacy specialist that reviews all regulator-facing copy? Nothing goes to SAHPRA/SAPC without it.
DGMP status. The SAHPRA GMP application status feeds directly into the SAHPRA conversation — confirm current standing.
EPharmacy registration. The SAPC side assumes a registered pharmacy + responsible pharmacist. Confirm the pharmacy's SAPC standing and who the dispensing pharmacist of record is.
