2 · Inventory module — replace the paper

She photographed the entire paper set. It reduces to three form shapes (more products still coming — "ek sal fotos van als neem").

11 products so far: Biodyne · Spliff · Pyrol · Diatomaceous Earth · Bittermag · Agrisil · Rooting Gel · Vectobac (Bacillus) · Cerasulfur · Neudosan · Hypochlorous Acid (HOCL).		2 d

Self-contained and high value — it disturbs nothing else in the system, and it kills a whole drawer of paper. Also a compliance win: a chemical batch + expiry tied to the plants it touched is exactly what an inspector asks for.

3 · Room-centric restructure — the batch-number golden thread

Today the app is organised by function (Activity Log, Env Log, Cleaning, Tasks). She wants it organised by room, with each room holding its own paperwork, and every record stamped with the batch number.

#RequirementEst.
3.1Room model + navigation. Cultivation: GH1 (Bays 1–3 → rows), GH2 (2 bays), Motherbay 1, Motherbay 2, Cloning. Processing: wet-material receiving · dry room 1 · dry room 2 · trimming · weighing & packaging · wash bay · chemical storage · receiving · bulk storage · final storage · storage room · sample room · rejected goods · dispatch · 2 hallways.3 d
3.2Each room carries its own logs — Temp & Humidity · Activity · Scouting · Daily checklist · Cleaning (daily/weekly/after-batch) · Culling · Mortality · Defoliation.3 d
3.3Re-parent existing data to rooms (activity, env, cleaning, mortality already exist — this is re-parenting, not rebuilding).2 d
3.4New logs: Scouting, Defoliation (batch #, strain, weight), Culling by batch. (Fields not in her photos — needs her input.)2 d
3.5Batch # golden thread — every record in every room stamps the batch; one continuous chain clone → veg → flower → harvest → processing → dispatch, viewable end-to-end.3 d
3.6Per row: Batch # and amount harvested per row.1 d
This is the real prize. The batch-number thread is seed-to-sale traceability — it is precisely the chain a SAHPRA inspector follows. Most of the underlying data already exists, so this is largely re-parenting and re-navigating what we have, plus a few new logs. Build the room shell + two rooms first, prove it with her, then roll the rest. Do not big-bang it.
