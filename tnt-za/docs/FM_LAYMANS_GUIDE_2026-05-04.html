Plain English · what's new, what to click
04 May 2026 · v1.0

Renae, this is your daily driver. Here's what changed.

Same farm, same Berne, same Lourens. But your dashboard now does more of the thinking for you. Less scrolling, fewer chase-ups, no more guessing what's overdue. This guide walks through what you'll see when you log in tomorrow morning, and what each new bit is for. No tech jargon.

R
Login URL: open the browser, go to your dashboard. It already knows you're Renae the Facility Manager. Your screen is set up around your job — operations, tickets, today's work. You won't see Berne's sign-off queue or Ilse's owner brief. You see your stuff.

01What you'll see at the top

First thing on the screen — the bits that matter today.

1 · Greeting + the time

Just "Good morning, Renae" with the clock. Confirms it's logged in as you.

2 · Status bar — "is anything on fire?"

A coloured stripe right under your name. Three things it might say:

  • "ALL CLEAR" — green. Nothing critical, nothing past deadline. You can take your coffee.
  • "2 SLA BREACH" — amber. Tickets have been sitting too long. Worth a look but not yet a fire.
  • "1 CRITICAL · 2 SLA BREACH" — red. Something needs attention now. The stripe also tells you how many open tickets and anomalies there are.

3 · Top 3 action cards

The 3 most urgent things you can act on today. Critical first, then anything past its time-to-resolve. Click any of them to jump into the ticket.

02The chat bubble (bottom-right)

Click the green bubble. Ask anything in plain words.

There's a small green chat circle in the corner of every page. It's like asking a colleague who knows everything about the farm — but they answer in 5–10 seconds, day or night.

What you can ask

  • "What's overdue right now?"
  • "Who hasn't signed off on the C.3.5 water section?"
  • "How many plants are in the flowering phase?"
  • "What does Lou have on his plate today?"
  • "Which calibrations are due this week?"

The little badge at the top of every reply tells you which AI answered. The "Grounded in live data" line tells you which numbers it used — so you can trust them, not guess.

Try it once: open the chat, type "what's burning today?", hit send. First answer comes back in about 6 seconds. After that you'll wonder how you managed without it.

03Tickets — your operational queue

Same place as before, but now smarter.

SLA timer (the little clock next to each ticket)

Every ticket has a maximum time it should stay open before someone acts on it:

  • Critical — 4 hours
  • High — 24 hours
  • Medium — 3 days
  • Low — 1 week

If a ticket is past its deadline, the timer turns red with a 🔥 flame icon. No more digging through old tickets — they call out for you.

Three tabs at the top — All / Mine / Reported

  • All — every open ticket on the farm
  • Mine — only the ones assigned to you (badge shows the count)
  • Reported — only the ones you raised (good for tracking your own follow-ups)

"Assign to person OR department"

When you create a new ticket, the assignee dropdown now has two sections:

  • 👤 Specific person — pick Lou, Sipho, JR, anyone
  • 🏢 Department / role — pick "any Cultivator" or "any Trimmer". Whoever in that role picks it up first owns it.
Use department assignment when you don't care who does it as long as someone in that team picks it up. Use person assignment when you specifically need that person.

05Bottleneck Radar

Where queues are getting stuck.

Below the tickets section, you'll see a "Bottleneck Radar" panel. It scans 5 queues — RP sign-off, Owner approvals, Maintenance, Lab backlog, Dispatch — and tells you which ones are slowing down.

If everything is moving, you'll see a quiet "All queues clear · no items past SLA" line. If something is stuck, you'll see a row like:

⚠ Maintenance
3 ageing · oldest 2d · SLA 24h 3

Click the row to jump straight to those tickets. The radar tells you where to look, not what to do — that's still your call.

06The "drift" thing — when AI flags an SMF section

It might land in your queue. Here's what that means.

The Site Master File (SMF) is the document SAHPRA wants. It describes the whole site — your floor plan, your equipment, your staff. The system now watches operational changes and tells Berne when something has changed enough that the SMF is out of date.

For you, this matters because some changes start with you. The clearest example: you add a new greenhouse → the SMF floor-plan section becomes "stale" → an SMF_DRIFT ticket is raised → it lands in Berne's queue, but you might be CC'd or asked to provide updated floor-plan info.

You don't need to do anything special. Just keep doing your job, and the system raises the right paperwork at the right time. Berne handles the redraft (with the AI helping her). All you need to know: "if my action triggered an SMF ticket, Berne knows about it."

07Quick wins for your morning routine

Five things to do in the first 10 minutes when you log in.
  1. Glance at the status bar. Green = good morning. Red = read the 3 action cards before anything else.
  2. Open the chat bubble, ask "what's burning today?" Get a 6-second summary so you don't have to scroll.
  3. Hit the "Mine" tab on tickets. See what's specifically on your plate. Resolve or reassign anything that's not really yours.
  4. Check the Compliance section for red cards. Calibration overdue? Schedule it before the day gets busy.
  5. Look at the Bottleneck Radar. If a queue is ageing, you might unblock it with one phone call.

08Things you do NOT have to do

The system handles these for you. Don't waste time on them.
  • Manually update the SMF. Berne owns it. The system tells her when it's stale.
  • Check who's overdue on what. The Bottleneck Radar shows it.
  • Hunt through old tickets to find the critical ones. SLA timers + Hero status bar surface them automatically.
  • Email staff to remind them about tickets. Their dashboard already shows their queue.
  • Calculate compliance metrics. The 9-card panel does it live.

09If something looks wrong

First port of call.
  • Numbers look off? Open the chat, ask "where does the number 42% quota come from?". The AI will tell you which DB rows it pulled.
  • Ticket assigned to wrong person? Open it, click the assignee dropdown, pick the right person or department. Done.
  • Compliance card showing red and you don't know why? Click the card. It opens the underlying record (calibration list, deviation page, etc.).
  • The chat says something that doesn't match what you know? The "Grounded in live data" footer shows what facts it used. Often the answer is there — your knowledge is more current than the database. Update the database (resolve the anomaly, close the ticket, mark the calibration done) and the chat will catch up.
  • Anything truly broken? Calvin (IT) for tech issues. Flo for platform problems. Berne for compliance questions. Lourens or Ilse for cultivation strategy.
The system always tells the truth about what it knows. If something seems wrong, the database is probably out of date — not the AI. The fix is usually one click, not a panic.
