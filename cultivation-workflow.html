How every form now works — the rail

1 · Auto-scheduled
The form fires itself on its cadence (daily checks, temp/humidity, cleaning) — no one prints or hands out paper.
2 · Captured on a tablet
The team taps the reading/count and signs with a PIN. No re-keying, no lost sheets.
3 · Self-policing
Overdue or missing items escalate automatically; the manager only sees the exceptions.
4 · Self-filing evidence
Every completion is hash-chained and filed as EU-GMP inspection evidence — instantly audit-ready.
The shift for the team: print → hand out → chase → check → file  →  it appears → capture → sign. Done.

Nursery Manager PWA

The source of input · where the data is born

Every number that flows through the platform starts here. The Nursery Manager works from a phone or tablet in the clone room — an installable app (PWA) that captures the cloning phase at the point of action. Nothing is written on paper and re-keyed later; the data is born digital, at the source.

What the NM captures — and what it hydrates

▸ Start a cloning job
Mother → strain + batch # auto → generates the batch, clone tray, and the whole 18-task schedule.
▸ Daily Temp & Humidity
3 readings/day vs target → hydrates the environmental record + flags out-of-range.
▸ Mortality & culling
Count + reason + photo → feeds yield/loss analytics + QA review.
▸ Sign-off on the spot
PIN signature → becomes hash-chained EU-GMP evidence instantly.

Who Drives, Who Executes

One schedule · one accountable owner per task

Every cultivation task has a clear chain: the Head of Cultivation drives the plan, the system generates the work, and the role that performs it executes. Nobody hands out paper; nobody guesses who's responsible.

🎯 DRIVER — Head of Cultivation
Lou (Lourens Eksteen) plans the phase in the Grow Calendar. Owns the Cloning Schedule & Harvest Request. Start a batch → the whole task set generates automatically.
✅ EXECUTOR — Nursery Manager
The NM executes what the schedule generates — the daily clone-room care — on the NM PWA (phone). He doesn't plan; he captures-at-source.
